\begin{abstract}
Between infinite, flat, ideally conducting parallel plates in $(3+1)$-dimensional Minkowski spacetime, the renormalized electromagnetic Casimir stress-energy tensor $\langle T_{\mu\nu}\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$ is conformally traceless ($T^\mu{}_\mu = 0$) and invariant under in-plane Lorentz boosts ($\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + P_\parallel = 0$). Coupled to semiclassical Einstein gravity, $G_{\mu\nu} = (8\pi G/c^4)\langle T_{\mu\nu}\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$, the canonical planar vacuum therefore sources zero local Ricci scalar curvature ($R = 0$), emits no bulk scalar-tensor breathing radiation, and carries zero non-dissipative angular momentum under rigid in-plane rotation. Here we formulate the semiclassical gravitational field theory of an idealized zero-temperature \emph{Canonical Prefractal Sector Cavity} (CPSC)---an azimuthally sectored, radially self-similar boundary configuration that breaks continuous scale invariance into discrete scale invariance (scale ratio $b$) while breaking planar boost symmetry and modulating vacuum boundary conditions between open-aperture saddle-point fringing and parallel-sector confinement. Combining the effective macroscopic trace formalism for self-similar hierarchies with first-principles three-dimensional boundary-element scattering and four-pass Euclidean correlator reconstructions across conformally coupled scalar ($\xi = 1/6$), minimally coupled scalar ($\xi = 0$), and perfect-conductor vector electromagnetic fields in Planck-normalized dimensionless units ($\epsilon_P = \ell_P / L$), we establish five purely theoretical gravitational phenomena: (i) symmetry-filtered spin-2 gravitational radiation from a $(6,4)$ Vernier cavity whose rigid $C_6$ and $C_4$ boundaries possess zero classical mass quadrupole ($\mathcal{I}_{ij}^{\mathrm{mass}} = \FcgGwRigidCSixQuadrupoleResidual$), yielding dimensionless vacuum-sourced strain $\tilde{h}_+ = \FcgGwVernierStrainAmpEmGenThree$ ($\FcgGwVernierCantorToPeriodicRatio\times$ relative to a single-scale periodic grating), alongside monopole ($l=0$) scalaron radiation ($\tilde{\varphi}_0 = \FcgGwScalaronMonopoleAmpGenThree$) in linearized $R + \alpha R^2$ gravity; (ii) an axial-force-free curvature crossover at $\theta_0 = \FcgFfcThetaZeroDegGenThree^\circ$ where net axial Casimir force vanishes ($|\tilde{F}_z| = \FcgFfcResidualForceAtThetaZero$) while the scale-locked effective Ricci scalar remains finite ($\tilde{R}_{\mathrm{eff}} = \FcgFfcRicciEffAtThetaZero$); (iii) a discrete-scale-invariant, log-periodic Newtonian potential and tidal curvature comb with complex scaling exponents $2 + i(2\pi k/\ln b)$ ($\hat{\omega}_1 = \FcgDsiRecoveredOmegaOneBThree$, $\hat{b} = \FcgDsiRecoveredScaleFactorBHat$); (iv) reversible rotary switching of local Null and Strong Energy Conditions ($\widetilde{\mathrm{NEC}}_z \in [\FcgEcNecZMinFacingPhase, \FcgEcNecZMaxOpenPhase]$) and Raychaudhuri geodesic focusing within Ford--Roman quantum energy inequality bounds (saturation ratio $\FcgEcFordRomanQeiSaturationRatio$); and (v) Cantor-stratified gravitomagnetic Lense--Thirring frame dragging ($\max|\tilde{\Omega}_{\mathrm{LT},z}^{\mathrm{vac}}| = \FcgGemPeakLenseThirringGenThree$) sourced by broken vacuum boost invariance. Although suppressed in physical magnitude by $\epsilon_P^2 = \hbar G/(c^3 L^2) \sim 10^{-58}$ at micrometer scales, these results demonstrate that boundary discrete scale invariance and azimuthal symmetry breaking qualitatively reorganize every sector of linearized semiclassical gravity.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Literature Review}
\label{sec:intro}

The Coupling between quantum vacuum fluctuations and the classical geometry of spacetime lies at the heart of semiclassical gravity~\cite{birrell1982,wald1984},
\begin{equation}
G_{\mu\nu} \equiv R_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2} g_{\mu\nu} R = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4} \langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu} \rangle_{\mathrm{ren}},
\label{eq:semiclassical_einstein}
\end{equation}
where $\langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu} \rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$ denotes the renormalized expectation value of the quantum stress-energy tensor in a specified boundary and metric state (using metric signature $(-,+,+,+)$ throughout~\cite{misner1973,wald1984}). Since Casimir's discovery that conducting boundaries modify electromagnetic zero-point modes~\cite{casimir1948,bordag2009}, the Casimir apparatus has served as the canonical theoretical arena in which renormalized quantum stresses are finite, calculable from first principles, and explicitly non-classical. Yet nearly everything known about how Casimir vacua gravitate has been derived from maximally symmetric Euclidean geometries---most notably two infinite, flat, parallel plates separated by distance $a$.

\subsection{Conformal Tracelessness and Boost Symmetry of the Planar Casimir Vacuum}
In their foundational image-method calculation, Brown and Maclay~\cite{brown1969} proved that for the source-free quantized electromagnetic field between ideal parallel conducting plates at $z=0$ and $z=a$, conformal symmetry and translational/boost invariance in the $(x,y)$ plane uniquely fix the renormalized stress-energy tensor to the spatially uniform form
\begin{equation}
\langle T^\mu{}_\nu \rangle_{\mathrm{flat}} = \frac{\pi^2 \hbar c}{720 a^4} \, \mathrm{diag}(-1, +1, +1, -3),
\label{eq:brown_maclay}
\end{equation}
where $T_{00} = +\rho_{\mathrm{vac}} c^2 = -\pi^2\hbar c/(720 a^4) < 0$ is the negative vacuum energy density, $T_{xx} = T_{yy} = P_\parallel = -\rho_{\mathrm{vac}} c^2 > 0$ are the transverse tensions, and $T_{zz} = P_\perp = 3\rho_{\mathrm{vac}} c^2 < 0$ is the attractive normal pressure. For curved boundaries or scalar fields with non-conformal curvature coupling $\xi \neq 1/6$~\cite{callan1970,deutsch1979,milton2016}, local surface divergences and bulk improvement terms $\propto (1/6 - \xi)\Box\langle\hat{\phi}^2\rangle$ enter $\langle T_{\mu\nu}(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$, whereas in curved four-dimensional backgrounds pure Weyl trace anomalies arise only at quadratic order in curvature~\cite{duff1994,birrell1982}.

Within linearized semiclassical gravity around flat spacetime, Eq.~\eqref{eq:brown_maclay} imposes three severe structural degeneracies on planar Casimir gravitation:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \emph{Vanishing Ricci scalar curvature}: Because the classical and renormalized Maxwell stress tensor is traceless in bulk vacuum ($T \equiv g^{\mu\nu}T_{\mu\nu} = T^\mu{}_\mu = -\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + 2P_\parallel + P_\perp = 0$), contracting Eq.~\eqref{eq:semiclassical_einstein} yields $R = -(8\pi G/c^4)\langle T^\mu{}_\mu\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}} = 0$ identically throughout the planar gap. Moreover, in quadratic $f(R) = R + \alpha R^2$ or scalar-tensor extensions of general relativity~\cite{starobinsky1980,damour1992,defelice2010,berry2011}, the massive or massless scalar gravitational degree of freedom (scalaron) couples exclusively to $T^\mu{}_\mu$ and therefore decouples from bulk conformal Casimir fluctuations.
    \item \emph{Algebraic locking of force, energy, and energy-condition violation}: Because $P_\perp = 3\rho_{\mathrm{vac}} c^2 < 0$, the planar Casimir force cannot vanish at finite separation without the vacuum energy also vanishing. Along the plate normal $k^\mu = (1,0,0,1)$, the Null Energy Condition (NEC) projection $T_{\mu\nu}k^\mu k^\nu = \rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + P_\perp = 4\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 < 0$ and the Strong Energy Condition (SEC) scalar $R_{00}(c^4/4\pi G) = \rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + \sum_i P_i = 2\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 < 0$ are unconditionally negative, forcing purely defocusing Raychaudhuri geodesic perturbations~\cite{raychaudhuri1955,kontou2020}.
    \item \emph{In-plane Lorentz boost invariance and rotational silence}: Parallel to the plates, the vacuum enthalpy density vanishes identically, $\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + P_\parallel = 0$. Under a tangential Lorentz boost or rigid rotation of axisymmetric disks, a planar Casimir state acquires zero first-order non-dissipative momentum density $T^{0\phi}_{\mathrm{vac}} \propto v_\phi(\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + P_\parallel) = 0$ (leaving only higher-order radiative vacuum friction $\propto \Omega^3$ on polarizable bodies~\cite{manjavacas2010,maghrebi2012}), and therefore generates zero stationary gravitomagnetic Lense--Thirring frame dragging~\cite{ciufolini2004,mashhoon2007}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Equivalence Principle, Vacuum Weight, and Energy Inequalities}
A second pillar of the literature has clarified how bounded Casimir cavities behave in external gravitational fields and under covariant conservation $\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{tot}} = 0$. Early calculations in Fermi and Rindler coordinates~\cite{bimonte2006,sorge2005} highlighted apparent orientation-dependent buoyancy forces when integrating $\langle T_{\mu\nu}\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$ in isolation. In the landmark ``How does Casimir energy fall?'' series, Fulling, Milton, Parashar, Romeo, Shajesh, and Wagner~\cite{fulling2007,milton2014fall} proved via variational energy-momentum principles that once the mechanical constraint stresses required to hold the plates at fixed separation are included covariantly, anisotropic spatial pressures integrate to zero by the relativistic virial (Laue) theorem, so that both the passive and active gravitational mass of a static, self-equilibrated Casimir apparatus equal $E_C/c^2$ in exact compliance with Einstein's Weak Equivalence Principle (WEP). Experimental proposals such as the \emph{Archimedes} balance~\cite{calloni2014} aim to weigh vacuum condensation energy shifts directly, while Sorge and Wilson~\cite{sorge2005,sorge2019} showed that genuine background Riemann tidal curvature ($\Phi'' \neq 0$, as near a Schwarzschild black hole) induces physical, gauge-invariant shifts in Casimir energy beyond uniform acceleration.

Concurrently, to prevent local Casimir violations of classical energy conditions from enabling macroscopic causal pathologies, Ford and Roman~\cite{ford1995,ford1997} and Fewster and Eveson~\cite{fewster1998,fewster2012} established rigorous Quantum Energy Inequalities (QEIs), proving that along any timelike worldline with smooth sampling function $f(t)$ of width $\tau$, renormalized negative energy densities satisfy $\int_{-\infty}^\infty \langle T_{00}(t)\rangle |f(t)|^2 \dd t \ge -C/\tau^4$, while background-potential studies by Graham and Olum~\cite{graham2003} demonstrated that negative Casimir densities are accompanied by positive stress-energy concentrations near boundaries.

\subsection{Discrete Scale Invariance, Aperture Stress Inversion, and Scope of This Work}
Two developments outside gravitational physics suggest a route to lifting all three planar degeneracies simultaneously in a purely theoretical setting. First, whereas mirror-symmetric bodies in vacuum always attract by the Kenneth--Klich theorem~\cite{kennethklich2006}, Levin \emph{et al.}~\cite{levin2010} demonstrated using surface-integral scattering~\cite{reid2009,reid2013} and time-domain stress integration~\cite{rodriguez2009pra} that positioning an elongated conductor inside or near an open metallic slot aperture in true vacuum ($\varepsilon = 1$) bends transverse electric fringing fields across the slot walls, creating a saddle-point energy landscape with repulsive axial force ($F_z > 0$, positive normal stress $P_z > 0$). Second, studies of quantum fields on fractals and self-similar plate stacks~\cite{akkermans2010,shajesh2016,shajesh2017} show that replacing continuous scale invariance with discrete scale invariance (DSI)~\cite{sornette1998}---a symmetry known in general relativity since Choptuik's discovery of log-periodic universality in critical gravitational collapse~\cite{choptuik1993} and Calcagni's multifractal quantum-gravity program~\cite{calcagni2010}---induces log-periodic spectral oscillations at frequencies $\omega_k = 2\pi k/\ln b$. Recently, Gordon~\cite{gordon2026prd} formulated an \emph{effective macroscopic trace framework} for self-similar Casimir hierarchies, proving that logarithmic scale running of the dimensionless Casimir coefficient $\mathcal{C}(\ln(d/\ell_*))$ generates a non-vanishing coarse-grained effective trace $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}} \propto -\partial_{\ln d}\mathcal{C}$ at the level of cavity thermodynamics.

In this paper, we unite the effective trace framework~\cite{gordon2026prd} with three-dimensional prefractal boundary scattering and linearized general relativity. To formulate a completely self-contained relativistic field-theory *gedankenexperiment* at $T = 0\text{ K}$, we introduce the \emph{Canonical Prefractal Sector Cavity} (CPSC): an idealized, dimensionless cylindrical boundary hierarchy in true vacuum whose azimuthal sector symmetry $(M_{\mathrm{up}}, M_{\mathrm{low}})$ and radial Cantor scaling ratio $b$ are chosen according to gravitational multipole selection rules, and whose fields are analyzed across conformal scalar ($\xi = 1/6$), minimal scalar ($\xi = 0$), and ideal perfect-conductor (PEC) vector electromagnetism alongside covariant constraint stresses ($\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{tot}} = 0$).

We derive and formulate numerical benchmarks for five gravitational phenomena:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Symmetry-Filtered Spin-2 and Spin-0 Gravitational Radiation (Sec.~\ref{sec:gw})}: Pairing a rotating $C_6$ upper boundary with a stationary $C_4$ prefractal aperture plate eliminates all classical rigid-body $l=2$ mass-quadrupole radiation while generating a rotating $m = 6-4 = 2$ Casimir vacuum energy-stress wave that radiates pure spin-2 gravitational waves ($h_+, h_\times$) at $\omega_{\mathrm{GW}} = 6\Omega$; concurrently, a $(4,4)$ cavity radiates $l=0$ scalar-tensor breathing waves via the time-varying effective trace $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}}(t)$.
    \item \textbf{Force-Free Spacetime Curvature and Tolman--Komar Mass Modulation (Sec.~\ref{sec:force_free})}: Across the angular transition between open-aperture repulsion ($F_z > 0$) and facing-sector attraction ($F_z < 0$), the axial force vanishes at $\theta = \theta_0$ while locking $\partial_{\ln|\tilde{z}|}\mathcal{C} = 3\mathcal{C} \neq 0$, creating an axial-force-free curvature source ($R_{\mathrm{eff}}(\theta_0) \neq 0$) while globally respecting the Weak Equivalence Principle ($\Delta M_{\mathrm{Komar}} = \Delta E_C/c^2$).
    \item \textbf{Discrete-Scale-Invariant Log-Periodic Spacetime Metric (Sec.~\ref{sec:metric_comb})}: In a homothetically $(r,z)$-staged prefractal cavity, solving $\nabla^2 \bar{h}_{\mu\nu} = -(16\pi G/c^4)\langle T_{\mu\nu}\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$ yields a Newtonian potential perturbation $h_{00}$ and tidal Riemann curvature $R_{0z0z}$ with complex scaling exponents $2 + i(2\pi k/\ln b)$ that can be switched on and off by rotation.
    \item \textbf{Rotary NEC/SEC Switching, Raychaudhuri Geodesic Lensing, and Worldline QEIs (Sec.~\ref{sec:energy_conditions})}: Rotating the CPSC switches local Null and Strong Energy Conditions between defocusing violation and saddle-point restoration, reversing Raychaudhuri null and timelike geodesic focusing while obeying Ford--Roman worldline QEI bounds.
    \item \textbf{Cantor-Stratified Gravitomagnetic Frame Dragging (Sec.~\ref{sec:gem})}: Breaking azimuthal boost invariance yields $\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + P_\phi \neq 0$, so steady rotation sweeps nested Cantor rings of vacuum momentum $T^{0\phi}_{\mathrm{vac}}$ that source Lense--Thirring vorticity $\mathbf{B}_g^{\mathrm{vac}} = \nabla \times \mathbf{h}_0$, in sharp contrast to the boost-invariant flat-annulus control ($\mathbf{B}_g^{\mathrm{vac}} = 0$).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Planck--Casimir Dimensionless Units}
Let $L$ denote the outer radius of the cavity. Because zero-temperature Casimir stresses scale purely with $\hbar c / L^4$ while Einstein's gravitational coupling introduces $G/c^4$, their product defines the squared Planck length $\ell_P^2 = \hbar G / c^3 \approx 2.612 \times 10^{-70}\text{ m}^2$ and the dimensionless Planck--cavity ratio $\epsilon_P \equiv \ell_P / L$. To render all field equations universal and free of numerical underflow, we introduce dimensionless coordinates and fields (denoted by tildes):
\begin{align}
\tilde{x}^\mu &= \frac{x^\mu}{L}, \qquad \tilde{\kappa} = \kappa L, \qquad \tilde{E}_C = \frac{L}{\hbar c} E_C, \qquad \tilde{F}_z = \frac{L^2}{\hbar c} F_z, \nonumber\\
\tilde{T}_{\mu\nu} &= \frac{L^4}{\hbar c} \langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu} \rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}, \qquad \tilde{R}_{\mu\nu} = \frac{L^4}{\ell_P^2} R_{\mu\nu}, \qquad \tilde{\bar{h}}_{\mu\nu} = \frac{L^2}{\ell_P^2} \bar{h}_{\mu\nu},
\label{eq:planck_units}
\end{align}
where $\bar{h}_{\mu\nu} \equiv h_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2}\eta_{\mu\nu}\eta^{\alpha\beta}h_{\alpha\beta}$ is the trace-reversed metric perturbation in Lorenz gauge $\partial^\mu \bar{h}_{\mu\nu} = 0$. In these units, the linearized semiclassical Einstein equation reduces identically to parameter-free form:
\begin{equation}
\tilde{\Box} \tilde{\bar{h}}_{\mu\nu}(\tilde{x}) = \left(-\frac{\partial^2}{\partial \tilde{x}_0^2} + \tilde{\nabla}^2\right)\tilde{\bar{h}}_{\mu\nu}(\tilde{x}) = -16\pi \, \tilde{T}_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{tot}}(\tilde{x}),
\label{eq:dimensionless_einstein}
\end{equation}
with dimensionless Ricci scalar $\tilde{R} = -8\pi \tilde{T}^\mu{}_\mu$. Physical SI quantities at any chosen reference scale $L$ follow immediately via $h_{\mu\nu} = \epsilon_P^2 \tilde{h}_{\mu\nu}$ and $R = (\epsilon_P^2/L^2)\tilde{R}$.

\subsection{Quantum Field Models, Effective Trace Identity, and Covariant Constraint Conservation}
\label{subsec:effective_trace_covariant}

To isolate the role of field spin and conformal vs.\ non-conformal stress-tensor structure, we evaluate three massless zero-temperature quantum field theories satisfying ideal Dirichlet/perfect-conductor conditions on the CPSC boundaries $\partial\Omega = \partial\Omega_{\mathrm{up}} \cup \partial\Omega_{\mathrm{low}}$:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \emph{Conformal massless real scalar ($\xi = 1/6$)}: Equipped with the Callan--Coleman--Jackiw improved stress-energy tensor~\cite{callan1970,birrell1982},
    \begin{equation}
    \hat{T}_{\mu\nu}^{(\xi)} = \partial_\mu \hat{\phi}\,\partial_\nu \hat{\phi} - \frac{1}{2}\eta_{\mu\nu}(\partial\hat{\phi})^2 + \xi\bigl(\eta_{\mu\nu}\Box - \partial_\mu\partial_\nu\bigr)\hat{\phi}^2,
    \label{eq:scalar_tmunu}
    \end{equation}
    which at $\xi = 1/6$ satisfies $\eta^{\mu\nu}\hat{T}_{\mu\nu}^{(1/6)} = 0$ identically on shell ($\Box\hat{\phi}=0$) everywhere in the interior vacuum $\mathbb{R}^3 \setminus \partial\Omega$.
    \item \emph{Minimally coupled massless real scalar ($\xi = 0$)}: Possesses an intrinsic local bulk trace $\eta^{\mu\nu}\langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu}^{(0)}(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}} = -\langle(\partial\hat{\phi})^2\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}} = -\frac{1}{2}\Box\langle\hat{\phi}^2(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}} \neq 0$ whenever $\langle\hat{\phi}^2(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$ is spatially inhomogeneous~\cite{deutsch1979,milton2016}.
    \item \emph{Ideal $0\text{ K}$ PEC vector electromagnetism}: Governed by the renormalized Maxwell stress-energy tensor
    \begin{align}
    \langle \hat{T}_{00} \rangle_{\mathrm{EM}} &= \frac{1}{2}\left\langle \varepsilon_0 \mathbf{E}^2 + \mu_0 \mathbf{H}^2 \right\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}, \nonumber\\
    \langle \hat{T}_{ij} \rangle_{\mathrm{EM}} &= \frac{1}{2}\delta_{ij}\left\langle \varepsilon_0 \mathbf{E}^2 + \mu_0 \mathbf{H}^2 \right\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}} - \left\langle \varepsilon_0 E_i E_j + \mu_0 H_i H_j \right\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}},
    \label{eq:maxwell_tmunu}
    \end{align}
    which is pointwise traceless ($\eta^{\mu\nu}\langle\hat{T}_{\mu\nu}\rangle_{\mathrm{EM}} = 0$) in $\mathbb{R}^3 \setminus \partial\Omega$ while supporting vector TE/TM polarization couplings absent in scalar models.
\end{enumerate}

How does a non-vanishing \emph{Effective Trace} arise in conformal theories ($\xi=1/6$ and PEC EM) whose microscopic interior trace vanishes? \section{Covariant Virial Identity and Conservation of the Total Quadrupole Tensor}

In linearized general relativity around Minkowski spacetime, Lorenz gauge $\partial^\mu \bar{h}_{\mu\nu} = 0$ requires $\partial_\mu T^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{tot}} = 0$, or explicitly:
\begin{equation}

\label{eq:app_continuity}

\end{equation}
In the ideal rigid-support limit ($Y_{\mathrm{rigid}} \to \infty$), boundary displacements under Casimir pressure scale as $\delta\tilde{u} \sim \tilde{P}/Y_{\mathrm{rigid}} \to 0$, so the elastic strain energy vanishes ($\tilde{T}^{00}_{\mathrm{con,elas}} \sim \tilde{P}^2/Y_{\mathrm{rigid}} \to 0$) while the constraint stress $\tilde{T}^{ij}_{\mathrm{con}}$ remains finite ($\mathcal{O}(\tilde{P})$) to satisfy $\partial_j \tilde{T}^{ji}_{\mathrm{con}} = -\partial_j \tilde{T}^{ji}_{\mathrm{vac}} - \partial_{\tilde{t}}\tilde{T}^{0i}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ at the boundary walls! Conversely, during steady rotation $\theta(\tilde{t}) = \tilde{\Omega}\tilde{t}$ of the $(6,4)$ Vernier cavity, $\tilde{T}^{00}_{\mathrm{vac}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}, \tilde{t})$ itself possesses a time-dependent $m=2$ azimuthal wave~\eqref{eq:vernier_vacuum_wave} while $\tilde{T}^{00}_{\mathrm{mass}}$ of the rigid $C_6$ and $C_4$ bodies has zero $m=2$ harmonic; by Eq.~\eqref{eq:app_virial}, the non-zero $\ddot{\tilde{\mathcal{Q}}}_{ij}^{\mathrm{vac}}$ is accompanied by an exact dynamic imbalance $\int (\tilde{T}^{ij}_{\mathrm{vac}} + \tilde{T}^{ij}_{\mathrm{con}})^{\mathrm{TF}}\dd^3\tilde{x} = \frac{1}{2}\ddot{\tilde{\mathcal{Q}}}_{ij}^{\mathrm{vac}}$ driven by the $\partial_{\tilde{t}}\tilde{T}^{0i}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ inertial term of the sweeping vacuum enthalpy! Our numerical solver constructs $\tilde{T}^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{con}}$ explicitly via Helmholtz--Hodge projection of $-\partial_\mu \tilde{T}^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{vac}}$ onto the boundary support domain and verifies Eq.~\eqref{eq:app_virial} to machine precision ($\FcgGwVirialConservationRelErr$).
