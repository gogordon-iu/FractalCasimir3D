\begin{abstract}
Between infinite, flat, ideally conducting parallel plates in $(3+1)$-dimensional Minkowski spacetime, the renormalized electromagnetic Casimir stress-energy tensor $\langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu}\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$ is conformally traceless ($T^\mu{}_\mu = 0$) and invariant under in-plane Lorentz boosts ($\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + P_\parallel = 0$). Coupled to semiclassical Einstein gravity, $G_{\mu\nu} = (8\pi G/c^4)\langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu}\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$, the canonical planar vacuum therefore sources zero local Ricci scalar curvature ($R = 0$), decouples from scalar-tensor breathing modes in bulk vacuum, and carries zero non-dissipative angular momentum under rigid in-plane rotation. Here we formulate the active semiclassical gravitational backreaction of an idealized zero-temperature \emph{Canonical Prefractal Sector Cavity} (CPSC)---an azimuthally sectored, radially self-similar boundary hierarchy with short-distance length cutoff $\ell_* = L b^{-N}$ that breaks continuous scale invariance into discrete scale invariance (scale ratio $b$) while breaking planar boost symmetry and modulating boundary conditions between open-aperture saddle-point fringing and parallel-sector confinement. Strictly distinguishing the cavity-integrated macroscopic effective trace $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}}$ from local curved-spacetime conformal trace anomalies, we combine cyclic-Bloch three-dimensional boundary-element scattering and four-pass Euclidean correlator reconstructions across conformally coupled scalar ($\xi = 1/6$), minimally coupled scalar ($\xi = 0$), and perfect-conductor vector electromagnetic fields with spectral linearized Einstein, Starobinsky $f(R) = R + \alpha R^2$ ($\alpha > 0$), and gravito-electromagnetic solvers in Planck-normalized dimensionless units ($\epsilon_P = \ell_P / L$). Enforcing exact covariant conservation $\nabla_\mu(T^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{vac}} + T^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{con}}) = 0$, we establish five gravitational phenomena: (i) symmetry-filtered spin-2 gravitational radiation from a $(6,4)$ Vernier cavity whose rigid $C_6$ and $C_4$ boundaries possess zero classical mass quadrupole ($\mathcal{I}_{ij}^{\mathrm{mass}} = \FcgGwRigidCSixQuadrupoleResidual$), yielding dimensionless vacuum-sourced strain $\tilde{h}_+ = \FcgGwVernierStrainAmpEmGenThree$ ($\FcgGwVernierCantorToPeriodicRatio\times$ relative to an equal-area periodic grating), alongside monopole ($l=0$) scalaron radiation ($\tilde{\varphi}_0 = \FcgGwScalaronMonopoleAmpGenThree$, Yukawa-suppressed by $\FcgConvScalaronYukawaSuppressionHeavy$ above the Starobinsky mass threshold); (ii) an axial-force-free curvature crossover at $\theta_0 = \FcgFfcThetaZeroDegGenThree^\circ$ ($|\tilde{F}_z| = \FcgFfcResidualForceAtThetaZero$) where the scale-locked coarse-grained Ricci curvature remains finite ($\tilde{R}_{\mathrm{eff}} = \FcgFfcRicciEffAtThetaZero$) while asymptotic Tolman--Komar mass respects the Weak Equivalence Principle; (iii) a discrete-scale-invariant, log-periodic Newtonian potential and tidal curvature comb with complex scaling exponents $2 + i(2\pi k/\ln b)$ ($\hat{\omega}_1 = \FcgDsiRecoveredOmegaOneBThree$, $\hat{b} = \FcgDsiRecoveredScaleFactorBHat$); (iv) reversible rotary switching of local Null and Strong Energy Conditions ($\widetilde{\mathrm{NEC}}_z \in [\FcgEcNecZMinFacingPhase, \FcgEcNecZMaxOpenPhase]$) and Raychaudhuri geodesic focusing strictly within Ford--Roman and Fewster worldline quantum energy inequality bounds (saturation ratio $\FcgEcFordRomanQeiSaturationRatio$); and (v) Cantor-stratified gravitomagnetic Lense--Thirring frame dragging ($\max|\tilde{\Omega}_{\mathrm{LT},z}^{\mathrm{vac}}|/\tilde{\Omega} = \FcgGemPeakLenseThirringGenThree$) sourced by broken vacuum boost invariance. \end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Literature Review}
\label{sec:intro}

The coupling between quantum vacuum fluctuations and the classical geometry of spacetime lies at the heart of semiclassical gravity~\cite{birrell1982,wald1984},
\begin{equation}
G_{\mu\nu} \equiv R_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2} g_{\mu\nu} R = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4} \langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu} \rangle_{\mathrm{ren}},
\label{eq:semiclassical_einstein}
\end{equation}
where $\langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu} \rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$ denotes the renormalized expectation value of the quantum stress-energy tensor in a specified boundary and metric state. Throughout this work we retain physical constants $(\hbar, c, G)$ explicitly in dimensional equations before mapping systematically to dimensionless Planck--Casimir variables, and adopt the metric signature $(-,+,+,+)$~\cite{misner1973,wald1984}. Since Casimir's discovery that conducting boundaries modify electromagnetic zero-point modes~\cite{casimir1948,bordag2009}, the Casimir apparatus has served as the canonical arena in which renormalized quantum stresses are finite, calculable from first principles, and non-classical. Yet most results on how Casimir vacua gravitate derive from maximally symmetric planar plates separated by a single length scale $a$, or treat the cavity as a passive test probe inside an externally prescribed curved metric.

\subsection{Conformal Tracelessness, Trace Anomalies vs.\ Effective Traces, and Planar Boost Symmetry}
In their foundational image-method calculation, Brown and Maclay~\cite{brown1969} proved that for the source-free quantized electromagnetic field between ideal parallel conducting plates at $z=0$ and $z=a$ (with interaction energy per unit area $E_C/A = -\pi^2\hbar c/(720 a^3)$), conformal invariance and in-plane $(x,y)$ Poincaré symmetry uniquely fix the renormalized covariant and mixed stress-energy tensors in $(-,+,+,+)$ signature to
\begin{align}
\langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu} \rangle_{\mathrm{flat}} &= \frac{\pi^2 \hbar c}{720 a^4} \, \mathrm{diag}(-1, +1, +1, -3), \nonumber\\
\langle \hat{T}^\mu{}_\nu \rangle_{\mathrm{flat}} &= \mathrm{diag}\bigl(-\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2,\, P_\parallel,\, P_\parallel,\, P_\perp\bigr) \nonumber\\
&= \frac{\pi^2 \hbar c}{720 a^4} \, \mathrm{diag}(+1, +1, +1, -3),
\label{eq:brown_maclay}
\end{align}
where $T_{00} = +\rho_{\mathrm{vac}} c^2 = -\pi^2\hbar c/(720 a^4) < 0$ is the negative vacuum energy density (so $T^0{}_0 = -\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 > 0$), $T_{xx} = T_{yy} = P_\parallel = -\rho_{\mathrm{vac}} c^2 > 0$ are the transverse tensions, and $T_{zz} = P_\perp = 3\rho_{\mathrm{vac}} c^2 < 0$ is the attractive normal pressure. Summing the mixed components confirms exact conformal tracelessness: $T^\mu{}_\mu = -\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + 2P_\parallel + P_\perp = (1 + 1 + 1 - 3)\pi^2\hbar c/(720 a^4) = 0$.

At the outset, it is essential to draw a sharp conceptual distinction between three physically distinct trace concepts in quantum field theory:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \emph{Local curved-spacetime conformal (Weyl) trace anomalies}: In curved backgrounds, ultraviolet regularization breaks classical Weyl invariance and generates a local state-independent anomalous trace $\langle \hat{T}^\mu{}_\mu(x)\rangle_{\mathrm{anom}}$ proportional to geometric curvature invariants~\cite{birrell1982,duff1994}. As shown by Setare and Rezaeian~\cite{setare2000} for Dirichlet plates in two-dimensional curved backgrounds, the local two-dimensional trace anomaly ($\propto R$) links boundary and background vacuum stresses directly; in $(3+1)$ dimensions, however, the local Weyl anomaly is quadratic in Riemann/Weyl curvature~\cite{duff1994} and therefore vanishes identically ($\mathcal{O}(\epsilon_P^4)$) in flat Minkowski interior vacuum $\mathbb{R}^3 \setminus \partial\Omega$.
    \item \emph{Non-conformal local operator traces}: For scalar fields with non-conformal curvature coupling $\xi \neq 1/6$~\cite{callan1970,deutsch1979,milton2016}, bulk improvement terms $\propto (1/6 - \xi)\Box\langle\hat{\phi}^2(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$ produce a genuine local interior trace even in flat space whenever $\langle\hat{\phi}^2(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$ is spatially inhomogeneous.
    \item \emph{Coarse-grained macroscopic effective trace}: Recently, Gordon~\cite{gordon2026prd} introduced an \emph{effective trace framework} for self-similar Casimir hierarchies, proving that when a cavity possesses an internal geometric length cutoff $\ell_*$ such that the dimensionless Casimir coefficient $\mathcal{C}(\ln(d/\ell_*))$ runs logarithmically with plate separation $d$, the cavity-averaged macroscopic stress tensor acquires a non-vanishing \emph{integrated effective trace} $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}} = -(\hbar c/d^4)\partial_{\ln d}\mathcal{C}$. Crucially, as emphasized in Ref.~\cite{gordon2026prd}, $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}}$ is a coarse-grained thermodynamic / boundary-moment quantity over the cavity volume---not a local quantum trace anomaly violating $\eta^{\mu\nu}\langle\hat{T}_{\mu\nu}\rangle_{\mathrm{EM}} = 0$ at interior vacuum points.
\end{enumerate}

Within linearized semiclassical gravity around flat spacetime, Eq.~\eqref{eq:brown_maclay} imposes three structural degeneracies on canonical planar Casimir gravitation:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \emph{Vanishing Ricci scalar and scalaron source}: Contracting Eq.~\eqref{eq:semiclassical_einstein} yields $R = -(8\pi G/c^4)\langle \hat{T}^\mu{}_\mu\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}} = 0$ across the planar gap, and constant $\mathcal{C} = -\pi^2/720$ gives $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}} = 0$. In quadratic $f(R) = R + \alpha R^2$ or scalar-tensor gravity~\cite{starobinsky1980,damour1992,defelice2010,berry2011,pourhassan2025epjc}, the extra scalar degree of freedom (scalaron) couples to the stress-energy trace and therefore decouples from planar conformal vacuum fluctuations.
    \item \emph{Algebraic locking of force, energy, and energy-condition violation}: Because $P_\perp = 3\rho_{\mathrm{vac}} c^2 < 0$, the planar force cannot vanish at finite separation without the vacuum energy also vanishing. Along the plate normal $k^\mu = (1,0,0,1)$, the Null Energy Condition (NEC) projection $T_{\mu\nu}k^\mu k^\nu = \rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + P_\perp = 4\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 < 0$ and Strong Energy Condition (SEC) combination $(T_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2}g_{\mu\nu}T)u^\mu u^\nu = \frac{1}{2}(\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + \sum_i P_i) = \rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 < 0$ are unconditionally negative, forcing purely defocusing Raychaudhuri geodesic perturbations~\cite{raychaudhuri1955,kontou2020}.
    \item \emph{In-plane Lorentz boost invariance and rotational silence}: Parallel to the plates, the vacuum enthalpy density vanishes identically, $\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + P_\parallel = 0$. Under rigid rotation of axisymmetric parallel disks relative to asymptotic inertial observers, the planar Casimir state acquires zero first-order laboratory momentum density $T^{0\phi}_{\mathrm{vac}} \propto v_\phi(\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + P_\parallel) = 0$ (leaving only higher-order radiative vacuum friction $\propto \Omega^3$ on polarizable media~\cite{manjavacas2010,maghrebi2012}), and therefore sources zero stationary gravitomagnetic Lense--Thirring field~\cite{ciufolini2004,mashhoon2007,costa2014grg,costa2021universe}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Prior Literature on Gravitational Casimir Physics, Dynamic Backgrounds, and Energy Inequalities}
Extensive prior research has examined Casimir cavities placed inside externally prescribed gravitational fields. Early Fermi- and Rindler-coordinate analyses~\cite{sorge2005,bimonte2006} revealed apparent orientation-dependent forces when integrating $\langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu}\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$ without mechanical support stresses. Fulling, Milton, Parashar, Romeo, Shajesh, and Wagner~\cite{fulling2007,milton2014fall} resolved this via variational principles, proving that covariant conservation $\nabla_\mu(T^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{vac}} + T^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{con}}) = 0$ causes spatial pressures to cancel in global mass integrals by the relativistic virial (Laue) theorem, yielding passive and active gravitational mass $E_C/c^2$ in compliance with the Weak Equivalence Principle (WEP). Complementing the \emph{Archimedes} vacuum-weighing experiment~\cite{calloni2014}, Sorge~\cite{sorge2021buoyancy} applied the quasi-local Tolman and Komar mass formalisms to prove independently that a static Casimir cavity carries gravitational mass equal to $E_C/c^2$ and experiences a consistent Archimedes-type vacuum buoyancy force in weak gravity, while Sorge and Wilson~\cite{sorge2019,wilson2020prd} quantified tidal and nonequilibrium corrections during free fall.

More recently, time-dependent and stationary rotating background metrics have been shown to alter intracavity quantum states significantly. Sorge~\cite{sorge2023memory} demonstrated a \emph{gravitational memory of the Casimir effect}, wherein a transient anisotropic spacetime pulse leaves a permanent shift in cavity vacuum energy. De Oliveira, Moreira, and Céleri~\cite{deoliveira2026entropy} derived particle creation and parametric-amplification resonances in the dynamical Casimir effect (DCE) when an oscillating cavity interacts with a passing gravitational wave. In stationary curved geometries, Zhang~\cite{zhang2017plb} proved that background frame dragging (off-diagonal metric components $g_{0i}$ as in Kerr spacetime) renormalizes Casimir energy and thermal free energy, while Pourhassan~\cite{pourhassan2025epjc} and Garattini and Tzikas~\cite{garattini2025epjc} analyzed rotating Casimir wormholes and perturbative scalaron stability in $f(R) = R + \alpha R^2$ gravity. Crucially, Refs.~\cite{sorge2023memory,deoliveira2026entropy,zhang2017plb} address the \emph{passive test-field problem}---how an imposed external GW or Kerr metric modifies the quantum field inside a simple cavity---whereas Refs.~\cite{pourhassan2025epjc,garattini2025epjc} study self-consistent exotic wormhole throats. Conversely, any interpretation of negative Casimir energy as ``exotic matter'' is strictly constrained by Quantum Energy Inequalities (QEIs): Ford and Roman~\cite{ford1995,ford1997} and Fewster and Eveson~\cite{fewster1998,fewster2012} proved that along any smooth timelike worldline sampled over timescale $\tau$, negative energy densities satisfy $\int_{-\infty}^\infty \langle \hat{T}_{00}(t)\rangle |f(t)|^2 \dd t \ge -C/\tau^4$, preventing macroscopic causal pathologies while requiring positive compensating stress concentrations near boundaries~\cite{graham2003,kontou2020}.

\subsection{Fractal Casimir Precedents, Aperture Stress Inversion, and Novel Contributions}
In flat Minkowski spacetime without gravity, studies of quantum fields on fractals~\cite{akkermans2010} and self-similar plate configurations by Shajesh \emph{et al.}~\cite{shajesh2016,shajesh2017} established explicit recursion methods for Cantor-type plate stacks and Sierpinski geometries, uncovering alternating-sign finite-generation corrections and convergence plateaus as prefractal depth $N \to \infty$, while Gordon~\cite{gordon2026prd} established the one-dimensional thermodynamic effective trace identity under discrete scale invariance (DSI)~\cite{sornette1998}---a symmetry celebrated in general relativity since Choptuik's critical gravitational collapse~\cite{choptuik1993} and Calcagni's multifractal gravity~\cite{calcagni2010}. Independently, whereas mirror-symmetric bodies in vacuum always attract~\cite{kennethklich2006}, Levin \emph{et al.}~\cite{levin2010} proved via exact boundary-element scattering~\cite{reid2009,reid2013,rodriguez2009pra} that an elongated conductor aligned with a metallic slot aperture in vacuum ($\varepsilon = 1$) experiences repulsive saddle-point axial Casimir force ($F_z > 0$).

Unlike Refs.~\cite{shajesh2016,shajesh2017,gordon2026prd} (which evaluate static Casimir energies or 1D effective traces without solving gravitational field equations) and unlike Refs.~\cite{sorge2021buoyancy,sorge2023memory,zhang2017plb,deoliveira2026entropy} (which place planar cavities in external metrics), the present work solves the \emph{active three-dimensional semiclassical backreaction problem} for a rotating, azimuthally sectored prefractal cavity in flat background spacetime. Specifically, we introduce the zero-temperature \emph{Canonical Prefractal Sector Cavity} (CPSC) and establish the following five new gravitational results:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Active Vernier Spin-2 Radiation and Massive $f(R)$ Scalaron Dispersion (Sec.~\ref{sec:gw})}: Whereas de Oliveira \emph{et al.}~\cite{deoliveira2026entropy} and Sorge~\cite{sorge2023memory} study cavity excitation by an incoming GW, we prove from cyclic scattering group theory (Appendix~\ref{app:vernier}) that steady adiabatic rotation ($\Omega \ll \pi c/d_0$, zero photon creation) of a rigid $C_6$ rotor over a $C_4$ prefractal stator eliminates all classical rigid-body $l=2$ mass-quadrupole radiation while generating a rotating $m = 6-4 = 2$ vacuum energy wave that actively radiates spin-2 GWs at $6\Omega$, alongside $l=0$ Starobinsky scalaron waves ($\alpha > 0$) governed by Klein--Gordon Yukawa screening.
    \item \textbf{Axial-Force-Free Effective Curvature vs.\ Global Tolman--Komar WEP Recovery (Sec.~\ref{sec:force_free})}: Extending Sorge's Tolman/Komar analysis~\cite{sorge2021buoyancy} and Fulling \emph{et al.}~\cite{fulling2007} to repulsive-to-attractive aperture crossovers~\cite{levin2010}, we prove that at the zero-axial-force angle $\theta_0$ where vertical wall loads vanish ($F_z(\theta_0)=0$), logarithmic scale locking ($\partial_{\ln|z|}\mathcal{C} = 3\mathcal{C}$) preserves a finite interior coarse-grained curvature $R_{\mathrm{eff}}(\theta_0) \neq 0$ while global Komar mass strictly equals $E_C(\theta)/c^2$.
    \item \textbf{Discrete-Scale-Invariant Log-Periodic Newtonian Metric and Tidal Comb (Sec.~\ref{sec:metric_comb})}: Extending flat-space Cantor hierarchies~\cite{shajesh2016,gordon2026prd} to the 3D linearized Einstein Poisson equation, we demonstrate a switchable log-periodic gravitational potential $h_{00}$ and tidal Riemann comb $R_{0z0z}$ with complex scaling dimensions $2 + i(2\pi k/\ln b)$.
    \item \textbf{Rotary NEC/SEC Geodesic Switching Bounded by Worldline QEIs (Sec.~\ref{sec:energy_conditions})}: We show that mechanical rotation reversibly switches axial slot channels between Raychaudhuri defocusing ($\mathrm{NEC}_z < 0$) and converging microlensing ($\mathrm{NEC}_z > 0, \mathrm{SEC} > 0$) while strictly obeying Ford--Roman and Fewster worldline QEIs~\cite{ford1997,fewster2012}.
    \item \textbf{Active Vacuum Lense--Thirring Vorticity from Broken Boost Invariance (Sec.~\ref{sec:gem})}: Contrasting with external-metric frame dragging~\cite{zhang2017plb} and framed within Costa and Natário's gauge-invariant gravito-electromagnetic hierarchy~\cite{costa2014grg,costa2021universe}, we prove that azimuthal slot walls break vacuum boost symmetry ($\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + P_\phi \neq 0$), endowing the rotating CPSC with genuine laboratory-frame vacuum momentum $T^{0\phi}_{\mathrm{vac}}$ and Cantor-stratified Lense--Thirring precession $\bm{\Omega}_{\mathrm{LT}}^{\mathrm{vac}}$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Planck--Casimir Dimensionless Units}
Let $L$ denote the outer radius of the cavity (carrying physical dimension of length). Zero-temperature Casimir stresses scale with $\hbar c / L^4$ while Einstein's coupling introduces $G/c^4$; their product defines the squared Planck length $\ell_P^2 = \hbar G / c^3 \approx 2.612 \times 10^{-70}\text{ m}^2$ and dimensionless Planck--cavity ratio $\epsilon_P \equiv \ell_P / L$. To prevent floating-point underflow while preserving exact physical scaling, we define dimensionless tilde quantities:
\begin{align}
\tilde{x}^\mu &= \frac{x^\mu}{L}, \qquad \tilde{\kappa} = \kappa L, \qquad \tilde{E}_C = \frac{L}{\hbar c} E_C, \qquad \tilde{F}_z = \frac{L^2}{\hbar c} F_z, \nonumber\\
\tilde{T}_{\mu\nu} &= \frac{L^4}{\hbar c} \langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu} \rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}, \qquad \tilde{R}_{\mu\nu} = \frac{L^4}{\ell_P^2} R_{\mu\nu}, \qquad \tilde{\bar{h}}_{\mu\nu} = \frac{L^2}{\ell_P^2} \bar{h}_{\mu\nu},
\label{eq:planck_units}
\end{align}
where $\bar{h}_{\mu\nu} \equiv h_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2}\eta_{\mu\nu}\eta^{\alpha\beta}h_{\alpha\beta}$ is the trace-reversed metric perturbation in Lorenz gauge $\partial^\mu \bar{h}_{\mu\nu} = 0$. In these units, the linearized semiclassical Einstein equation takes the parameter-free form
\begin{equation}
\tilde{\Box} \tilde{\bar{h}}_{\mu\nu}(\tilde{x}) = \left(-\frac{\partial^2}{\partial \tilde{x}_0^2} + \tilde{\nabla}^2\right)\tilde{\bar{h}}_{\mu\nu}(\tilde{x}) = -16\pi \, \tilde{T}_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{tot}}(\tilde{x}),
\label{eq:dimensionless_einstein}
\end{equation}
with local dimensionless Ricci scalar $\tilde{R}(\tilde{x}) = -8\pi \tilde{T}^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{tot}}(\tilde{x})$. Physical SI values at any reference length scale $L$ follow via $h_{\mu\nu} = \epsilon_P^2 \tilde{h}_{\mu\nu}$ and $R = (\epsilon_P^2/L^2)\tilde{R}$.

\subsection{Geometry of the Canonical Prefractal Sector Cavity}
We consider two disjoint, rigid, ideally lossless boundary assemblies in $(3+1)$-dimensional Minkowski vacuum at $T = 0\text{ K}$ spanning the cylindrical annulus $r \in [r_{\mathrm{in}}, r_{\mathrm{out}}] = [0.25 L, 1.00 L]$ ($\tilde{r} \in [0.25, 1.00]$, radial span $\Delta\tilde{r} = 0.75$; Fig.~\ref{fig:cpsc_architecture}):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \emph{Recursive radial Cantor hierarchy $\mathcal{C}_{N,b}$ and cutoff length $\ell_*$}: Given prefractal generation order $N \in \{0,1,2,3,4\}$ and scaling factor $b > 2$ (retention ratio $\eta = 1/b$ and central removal fraction $\gamma = 1 - 2/b$, yielding the middle-third Cantor set at $b=3$), generation $m \in \{1,\dots,N\}$ defines $2^{m-1}$ concentric aperture slots ($N_{\mathrm{tracks}} = 2^N - 1$ total slots for $N \ge 1$) of dimensionless radial width
    \begin{equation}
    \tilde{W}_m = \Delta\tilde{r}\left(1 - \frac{2}{b}\right) b^{-(m-1)}, \qquad m = 1,\dots,N,
    \label{eq:cantor_widths}
    \end{equation}
    terminating at the physical short-distance prefractal cutoff length $\ell_* \equiv \Delta r\, b^{-N}$ (dimensionless $\tilde{\ell}_* = \ell_*/L = \Delta\tilde{r}\,b^{-N}$). Centered inside each slot $\tilde{r} \in [\tilde{r}_{c,k} - \tilde{W}_m/2, \tilde{r}_{c,k} + \tilde{W}_m/2]$ is an upper vane of width $\tilde{w}_m = \chi \tilde{W}_m$ ($\chi = 0.50$), yielding self-similar radial clearances $\tilde{g}_{r,m} = \frac{1}{2}(1-\chi)\tilde{W}_m = \tilde{g}_{r,1} b^{-(m-1)}$.
    \item \emph{Coplanar versus homothetic $(r,z)$ vertical staging}: In the \emph{coplanar} CPSC, lower aperture rims occupy $\tilde{z} \in [-\tilde{\tau}_0, 0]$ ($\tilde{\tau}_0 = 0.03$), upper vanes span $\tilde{z} \in [\tilde{z}_{\mathrm{vane}}, \tilde{z}_{\mathrm{vane}} + \tilde{h}_0]$ ($\tilde{h}_0 = 0.12$, baseline $\tilde{z}_{\mathrm{vane}} = -0.10$), and lower facing sectors sit at $\tilde{z}_{\mathrm{top}} = -0.12$ (vertical facing separation $\tilde{d}_0 = d_0/L = 0.02$). In the \emph{homothetic $(r,z)$-staged} CPSC, cross-sectional dimensions and vertical aperture centers scale geometrically as
    \begin{equation}
    \tilde{z}_{{\rm stage},m} = -\tilde{Z}_1 b^{-(m-1)}, \qquad \tilde{h}_{{\rm rim},m} = \tilde{\tau}_1 b^{-(m-1)},
    \label{eq:homothetic_staging}
    \end{equation}
    with $\tilde{Z}_1 = 0.27$ and $\tilde{\tau}_1 = 0.054$, so rescaling $|\tilde{z}| \to |\tilde{z}|/b$ maps generation $m$ onto generation $m+1$.

where every argument of $\mathcal{C}$ is manifestly dimensionless. \begin{align}

\label{eq:macroscopic_pressures_si}
\end{align}
Summing the mixed coarse-grained components $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}} \equiv -\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + P_r + P_\phi + P_z$ cancels the $3\mathcal{C} - \mathcal{C} - 2\mathcal{C} = 0$ algebraic terms identically, leaving in dimensional and dimensionless forms (with $\tilde{d} = |\tilde{z}|$):
\begin{align}

\label{eq:effective_trace_3d}
\end{align}
where $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{cav}} \equiv 1 + |\tilde{z}|\partial_{|\tilde{z}|}|_{\tilde{\ell}_*} + \tilde{R}\partial_{\tilde{R}}|_{\tilde{\ell}_*}$. Equation~\eqref{eq:effective_trace_3d} satisfies three exact consistency checks: (i) in the laterally translation-invariant 1D plate-stack limit ($\partial_{\ln R}\mathcal{C} = 0$), Eq.~\eqref{eq:effective_trace_3d} reduces identically---including sign and prefactor---to Gordon's formula $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}} = -(\hbar c/d^4)\partial_{\ln d}\mathcal{C}$~\cite{gordon2026prd}; (ii) for featureless parallel plates where $\mathcal{C} = -\pi^2/720 = \mathrm{const}$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:macroscopic_pressures_si} reproduces Casimir's $E_C/A = -\pi^2\hbar c/(720 d^3)$ law, $P_\parallel = -\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2$, $P_\perp = 3\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2$, and $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}} = 0$; and (iii) by Euler homogeneity under simultaneous dilation of all lengths including $\ell_*$, $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{cav}}\tilde{E}_C = -\tilde{\ell}_*\partial_{\tilde{\ell}_*}\tilde{E}_C$, demonstrating that $\tilde{T}^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}}$ originates from boundary work against the internal prefractal cutoff $\tilde{\ell}_*$. Note also how this $T = 0\text{ K}$ zero-point trace differs from the finite-temperature fractal thermal sector~\cite{akkermans2010,gordon2026prd}: for thermal radiation on a fractal of spectral dimension $d_s$ (the effective dimension governing diffusion and Laplacian eigenvalue density $\propto \omega^{d_s-1}$), the coarse-grained thermal energy density $\rho_{\mathrm{th}} = U_{\mathrm{th}}/V_s$ per spectral volume $V_s$ and isotropic pressure $p_{\mathrm{th}} = \rho_{\mathrm{th}}/d_s$ yield $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{th}} = -\rho_{\mathrm{th}} + 3p_{\mathrm{th}} = \rho_{\mathrm{th}}(3/d_s - 1)$. Because our CPSC operates strictly at $T = 0\text{ K}$ ($\rho_{\mathrm{th}} = 0$) in $(3+1)$-dimensional bulk vacuum, $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{th}}$ vanishes identically and only the zero-point logarithmic derivative~\eqref{eq:effective_trace_3d} is active. \label{eq:dsi_fourier}
\end{equation}

\label{eq:covariant_conservation}
\end{equation}

\subsection{3D Cyclic-Bloch Boundary-Element Scattering and Scale-Derivative Engine}
\label{subsec:methods_bem}

\begin{equation}

\label{eq:bem_logdet}
\end{equation}
where $\sigma_{\mathrm{field}} = \frac{1}{2}$ for a real scalar field and $\sigma_{\mathrm{field}} = 1$ for two-polarization electromagnetism, and $\mathbf{M}(\tilde{\kappa}) = \mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{UU}}^{-1}\mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{UL}}\mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{LL}}^{-1}\mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{LU}}$ subtracts single-body ultraviolet self-energies identically at every $\tilde{\kappa}$. Boundary surfaces are meshed into $N_p$ quadrilateral/annular panels $\{\mathcal{P}_a\}_{a=1}^{N_p}$ with piecewise-constant basis functions; diagonal self-interaction integrals are regularized analytically by replacing $|\tilde{\mathbf{r}}-\tilde{\mathbf{r}}'|$ at coincidence with the equal-area disk radius $\tilde{\sigma}_a = \sqrt{\tilde{A}_a/(16\pi)}$:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \emph{Scalar Dirichlet kernel}: Single-layer Euclidean Yukawa kernel $G_0(\tilde{\mathbf{r}}, \tilde{\mathbf{r}}'; \tilde{\kappa}) = \exp(-\tilde{\kappa}|\tilde{\mathbf{r}}-\tilde{\mathbf{r}}'|)/(4\pi|\tilde{\mathbf{r}}-\tilde{\mathbf{r}}'|)$ ($N_p$ degrees of freedom).
    \item \emph{PEC vector-dyadic kernel}: Electric-field integral equation (EFIE) projecting two tangential surface-current basis vectors $(\hat{\mathbf{t}}_{a,1}, \hat{\mathbf{t}}_{a,2})$ per panel onto the Euclidean dyadic Green's tensor $\mathbf{G}(\tilde{\mathbf{r}}, \tilde{\mathbf{r}}'; \tilde{\kappa}) = \bigl(\mathbf{I} - \tilde{\kappa}^{-2}\tilde{\nabla}\tilde{\nabla}\bigr) G_0(\tilde{\mathbf{r}}, \tilde{\mathbf{r}}'; \tilde{\kappa})$ ($2N_p$ degrees of freedom).
\end{itemize}
For constellations with cyclic greatest common divisor $M_g = \gcd(M_{\mathrm{up}}, M_{\mathrm{low}}) > 1$ ($M_g = 4$ for $(4,4)$ and $M_g = 2$ for $(6,4)$), discrete Fourier projection into $M_g$ decoupled Bloch blocks $\mathbf{M}^{(\ell)}(\tilde{\kappa}) = \sum_{q=0}^{M_g-1} e^{2\pi i \ell q / M_g} \mathbf{M}_{0,q}(\tilde{\kappa})$ accelerates LU factorization by $M_g^3$ without approximation (verified against full-ring dense LU to $<10^{-11}$ relative error). \label{subsec:methods_voxel}

\begin{equation}

\label{eq:scattered_greens_pointwise}
\end{equation}
Analytical spatial derivatives $\partial_i \partial'_j \Delta G_{\mathrm{ren}}|_{\tilde{\mathbf{x}}'=\tilde{\mathbf{x}}}$ and $\tilde{\kappa}^2 \Delta G_{\mathrm{ren}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}, \tilde{\mathbf{x}}; \tilde{\kappa})$ assemble the scalar~\eqref{eq:scalar_tmunu} and dyadic electromagnetic~\eqref{eq:maxwell_tmunu} stress tensors at each voxel.

\subsection{Role of Constraint Stresses and Elimination of Coordinate Artifacts}
Evaluating linearized Einstein or Komar integrals over $\langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu}\rangle_{\mathrm{vac}}$ without mechanical support stresses $\tilde{T}_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{con}}$ violates $\partial_\mu \tilde{T}^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{tot}} = 0$ and introduces gauge artifacts~\cite{bimonte2006,fulling2007,sorge2021buoyancy}. \begin{enumerate}
    \item In the $(6,4)$ Vernier radiator (Sec.~\ref{sec:gw}), elastic support stresses balance local pressures; however, because $\tilde{\mathcal{Q}}_{ij}^{\mathrm{tot}}$ integrates energy density $\tilde{T}_{00}$---where rigid $C_6$ and $C_4$ mass distributions have identically zero $m=2$ quadrupole and elastic strain energy scales as $\tilde{P}^2/Y_{\mathrm{rigid}} \to 0$ in the rigid limit---the $6\Omega$ spin-2 wave is genuinely sourced by the sweeping $m=2$ Casimir vacuum energy wave, verified to virial error $\FcgGwVirialConservationRelErr$.
    \item In the stationary Tolman--Komar sector (Sec.~\ref{sec:force_free}), exterior support stresses enforce $\int_{\mathbb{R}^3}\tilde{P}_{i,\mathrm{tot}}\dd^3\tilde{x} = 0$ globally so $\Delta\tilde{M}_{\mathrm{Komar}}(\infty) = \tilde{E}_C(\theta)$~\cite{fulling2007,sorge2021buoyancy}, while leaving interior coarse-grained curvature $\tilde{R}_{\mathrm{eff}}(\theta_0)$ at zero axial force unscreened inside the open gap.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Physical Scale Estimates and Analog-Gravity Connections}
Restoring physical constants at an illustrative outer radius $L = 1\ \mu\text{m}$ ($\epsilon_P^2 = \ell_P^2/L^2 = 2.612 \times 10^{-58}$) underscores the purely foundational nature of these effects in real spacetime: static metric perturbations scale as $|h_{\mu\nu}| = \epsilon_P^2 |\tilde{h}_{\mu\nu}| \sim 10^{-56}\text{--}10^{-54}$, coarse-grained Ricci curvatures scale as $|R_{\mathrm{eff}}| = (\epsilon_P^2/L^2)|\tilde{R}_{\mathrm{eff}}| \sim 10^{-43}\text{ m}^{-2}$, and Vernier strains scale as $h_+ \sim \epsilon_P^2 (L/r_{\mathrm{obs}})(\Omega L/c)^2 |\tilde{Q}_2|$, far below direct gravitational-wave detection thresholds even for synchronized $N_{\mathrm{arr}} \times N_{\mathrm{arr}}$ arrays. Nevertheless, in acoustic Bose--Einstein condensate and optical metamaterial analog-gravity platforms where effective propagation speeds $c_{\mathrm{eff}} \ll c$ and effective couplings replace $\ell_P$, analogous boundary-induced effective-trace and boost-breaking vorticity phenomena offer promising targets for laboratory simulation.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have formulated a self-contained semiclassical field-theoretic and numerical framework for prefractal Casimir gravitation at zero temperature, uniting three-dimensional boundary-element scattering with linearized Einstein, Starobinsky $f(R)$, and gravito-electromagnetic theory. Strictly separating the cavity-integrated macroscopic effective trace~\cite{gordon2026prd} from local curved-spacetime conformal trace anomalies~\cite{duff1994,setare2000} and enforcing covariant stress-energy conservation~\cite{fulling2007,sorge2021buoyancy}, we demonstrated five active gravitational backreaction phenomena in the Canonical Prefractal Sector Cavity (Table~\ref{tab:summary_five_effects}): (1) symmetry-filtered $6\Omega$ Vernier spin-2 gravitational waves and Yukawa-screened $l=0$ Starobinsky scalaron waves; (2) an axial-force-free macroscopic curvature crossover respecting global Tolman--Komar WEP mass recovery; (3) a switchable discrete-scale-invariant log-periodic Newtonian potential and tidal Riemann comb; (4) rotary NEC/SEC geodesic focusing/defocusing switching strictly bounded by Ford--Roman and Fewster worldline QEIs; and (5) Cantor-stratified Lense--Thirring frame dragging driven by broken azimuthal boost invariance.

Several caveats and future directions merit emphasis. First, our calculations assume idealized zero-temperature ($T=0\text{ K}$) lossless Dirichlet and PEC boundaries in the adiabatic slow-rotation regime ($\Omega L/c \ll 1$); incorporating finite-conductivity Lifshitz dielectric dispersion, nonzero temperature ($T > 0\text{ K}$, where the fractal thermal trace $\rho_{\mathrm{th}}(3/d_s - 1)$ enters~\cite{akkermans2010,gordon2026prd}), and non-adiabatic dynamical Casimir particle creation at $\Omega L/c \sim \mathcal{O}(1)$~\cite{deoliveira2026entropy} represents a natural theoretical extension. Second, while real-spacetime amplitudes are suppressed by $\epsilon_P^2 \sim 10^{-58}$ at micrometer length scales, mapping the Vernier selection rules and broken-boost enthalpy currents onto metamaterial and superfluid analog-gravity systems provides a concrete avenue for future experimental exploration.

\section{Covariant Virial Identity and Conservation of the Total Quadrupole Tensor}
\label{app:virial}

In linearized general relativity around Minkowski spacetime, Lorenz gauge $\partial^\mu \bar{h}_{\mu\nu} = 0$ requires $\partial_\mu T^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{tot}} = 0$:
\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial \tilde{T}^{00}_{\mathrm{tot}}}{\partial \tilde{t}} + \partial_j \tilde{T}^{j0}_{\mathrm{tot}} = 0, \qquad \frac{\partial \tilde{T}^{0i}_{\mathrm{tot}}}{\partial \tilde{t}} + \partial_j \tilde{T}^{ji}_{\mathrm{tot}} = 0.
\label{eq:app_continuity}
\end{equation}
Multiplying the energy continuity equation by $\tilde{x}^i\tilde{x}^j$, integrating over a ball $\mathcal{B}_R$ enclosing the isolated cavity-plus-support assembly ($\tilde{T}^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{tot}} = 0$ on $\partial\mathcal{B}_R$), and integrating by parts twice using momentum continuity yields the relativistic second-moment virial theorem:
\begin{equation}
\frac{\dd^2}{\dd\tilde{t}^2} \int_{\mathcal{B}_R} \tilde{T}^{00}_{\mathrm{tot}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}, \tilde{t}) \, \tilde{x}^i \tilde{x}^j \, \dd^3\tilde{x} = 2 \int_{\mathcal{B}_R} \tilde{T}^{ij}_{\mathrm{tot}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}, \tilde{t}) \, \dd^3\tilde{x}.
\label{eq:app_virial}
\end{equation}
In the ideal rigid-support limit ($Y_{\mathrm{rigid}} \to \infty$), elastic displacements scale as $\delta\tilde{u} \sim \tilde{P}/Y_{\mathrm{rigid}} \to 0$, so elastic strain energy vanishes ($\tilde{T}^{00}_{\mathrm{con,elas}} \sim \tilde{P}^2/Y_{\mathrm{rigid}} \to 0$) while constraint stresses $\tilde{T}^{ij}_{\mathrm{con}} \sim \mathcal{O}(\tilde{P})$ satisfy $\partial_j \tilde{T}^{ji}_{\mathrm{con}} = -\partial_j \tilde{T}^{ji}_{\mathrm{vac}} - \partial_{\tilde{t}}\tilde{T}^{0i}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ at boundary walls. For static configurations ($\partial_{\tilde{t}} = 0$), Eq.~\eqref{eq:app_virial} gives $\int \tilde{T}^{ij}_{\mathrm{con}}\dd^3\tilde{x} = -\int \tilde{T}^{ij}_{\mathrm{vac}}\dd^3\tilde{x}$ (Laue's theorem), ensuring $\Delta\tilde{M}_{\mathrm{Komar}}(\infty) = \tilde{E}_C$~\cite{fulling2007,sorge2021buoyancy}.
