Here we formulate the active semiclassical gravitational backreaction of an idealized zero-temperature \emph{Canonical Prefractal Sector Cavity} (CPSC)---an azimuthally sectored, radially self-similar boundary hierarchy of finite generation $N$ with short-distance length cutoff $\ell_* = \Delta r\, b^{-N}$ that endows the Casimir coefficient with discrete scale dependence (log-periodically running with scale ratio $b$) while breaking planar boost symmetry and modulating boundary conditions between open-aperture saddle-point fringing and parallel-sector confinement. Enforcing exact covariant conservation $\nabla_\mu(T^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{vac}} + T^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{con}}) = 0$ in the sub-relativistic rigid-support regime, we establish five gravitational results and delineate their exact relation to prior work: (i) symmetry-filtered spin-2 gravitational radiation from a $(6,4)$ Vernier cavity whose rigid $C_6$ and $C_4$ boundaries possess zero classical $l=2$ mass quadrupole ($\mathcal{I}_{ij}^{\mathrm{mass}} = \FcgGwRigidCSixQuadrupoleResidual$), yielding dimensionless vacuum-sourced strain $\tilde{h}_+ = \FcgGwVernierStrainAmpEmGenThree$ ($\FcgGwVernierCantorToPeriodicRatio\times$ relative to an equal-area periodic grating), alongside monopole ($l=0$) scalaron radiation ($\tilde{\varphi}_0 = \FcgGwScalaronMonopoleAmpGenThree$, Yukawa-suppressed by $\FcgConvScalaronYukawaSuppressionHeavy$ above the Starobinsky mass threshold); \section{Introduction and Literature Review}
\label{sec:intro}

The coupling between quantum vacuum fluctuations in quantum field theory (QFT) and the classical geometry of spacetime in general relativity (GR) lies at the heart of semiclassical gravity~\cite{birrell1982,wald1984},
\begin{equation}
G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} \equiv R_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2} g_{\mu\nu} R + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4} \langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu} \rangle_{\mathrm{ren}},
\label{eq:semiclassical_einstein}
\end{equation}
where $\langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu} \rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$ denotes the renormalized expectation value of the quantum stress-energy tensor in a specified boundary and metric state, and we set the background cosmological constant $\Lambda = 0$ in asymptotically flat Minkowski spacetime (noting, as emphasized by Cerdonio and Rovelli~\cite{cerdonio2015ijmpd}, that laboratory Casimir forces probe boundary-dependent vacuum energy differences rather than free-space $\Lambda$). Throughout this work we retain physical constants $(\hbar, c, G)$ explicitly in dimensional equations before mapping systematically to dimensionless Planck--Casimir variables, and adopt the metric signature $(-,+,+,+)$~\cite{misner1973,wald1984}. Since Casimir's discovery that conducting boundaries modify electromagnetic zero-point modes~\cite{casimir1948,bordag2009}, the Casimir apparatus has served as the canonical arena in which renormalized quantum stresses are finite, calculable from first principles, and non-classical. Yet most results on how Casimir vacua gravitate derive from maximally symmetric planar plates separated by a single length scale $a$, or treat the cavity as a passive test probe inside an externally prescribed curved metric.

\subsection{Conformal Tracelessness, Trace Anomalies vs.\ Effective Traces, and Planar Boost Symmetry}
In their foundational image-method calculation, Brown and Maclay~\cite{brown1969} proved that for the source-free quantized electromagnetic field between ideal parallel conducting plates at $z=0$ and $z=a$ (with interaction energy per unit area $E_C/A = -\pi^2\hbar c/(720 a^3)$, equal to twice the single massless real scalar result $\epsilon_{\mathrm{Cas}}^{\mathrm{scalar}} = -\pi^2\hbar c/(1440 a^4)$ due to two transverse photon polarizations~\cite{casimir1948,bordag2009}), conformal invariance and in-plane $(x,y)$ Poincaré symmetry uniquely fix the renormalized covariant and mixed stress-energy tensors in $(-,+,+,+)$ signature to
\begin{align}
\langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu} \rangle_{\mathrm{flat}} &= \frac{\pi^2 \hbar c}{720 a^4} \, \mathrm{diag}(-1, +1, +1, -3), \nonumber\\
\langle \hat{T}^\mu{}_\nu \rangle_{\mathrm{flat}} &= \mathrm{diag}\bigl(-\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2,\, P_\parallel,\, P_\parallel,\, P_\perp\bigr) \nonumber\\
&= \frac{\pi^2 \hbar c}{720 a^4} \, \mathrm{diag}(+1, +1, +1, -3),
\label{eq:brown_maclay}
\end{align}
where $T_{00} = +\rho_{\mathrm{vac}} c^2 = -\pi^2\hbar c/(720 a^4) < 0$ is the negative vacuum energy density (so $T^0{}_0 = -\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 > 0$), $T_{xx} = T_{yy} = P_\parallel = -\rho_{\mathrm{vac}} c^2 > 0$ are the transverse tensions, and $T_{zz} = P_\perp = 3\rho_{\mathrm{vac}} c^2 < 0$ is the attractive normal pressure. Summing the mixed components confirms exact conformal tracelessness: $T^\mu{}_\mu = -\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + 2P_\parallel + P_\perp = (1 + 1 + 1 - 3)\pi^2\hbar c/(720 a^4) = 0$.

At the outset, it is essential to draw a sharp conceptual and gravitational distinction between three physically distinct trace concepts in quantum field theory and clarify how each couples to spacetime geometry:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \emph{Local curved-spacetime conformal (Weyl) trace anomalies}: In curved backgrounds, ultraviolet regularization breaks classical Weyl invariance and generates a state-independent anomalous trace $\langle \hat{T}^\mu{}_\mu(x)\rangle_{\mathrm{anom}}$ proportional to geometric curvature invariants~\cite{birrell1982,duff1994}. As shown by Setare and Rezaeian~\cite{setare2000} for Dirichlet plates in two-dimensional curved backgrounds, the local two-dimensional trace anomaly ($\propto R$) links boundary and background vacuum stresses directly; in $(3+1)$ dimensions, however, the local Weyl anomaly is quadratic in Riemann/Weyl curvature~\cite{duff1994} and vanishes identically ($\mathcal{O}(\epsilon_P^4)$) in flat Minkowski interior vacuum $\mathbb{R}^3 \setminus \partial\Omega$.
    \item \emph{Non-conformal local operator traces}: For scalar fields with non-conformal curvature coupling $\xi \neq 1/6$~\cite{callan1970,deutsch1979,milton2016}, bulk improvement terms $\propto (1/6 - \xi)\Box\langle\hat{\phi}^2(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$ produce a genuine pointwise interior trace $\eta^{\mu\nu}\langle\hat{T}_{\mu\nu}(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}} \neq 0$ even in flat space whenever $\langle\hat{\phi}^2(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$ is inhomogeneous, sourcing a true pointwise interior Ricci scalar $R(\mathbf{x}) = -(8\pi G/c^4)\langle\hat{T}^\mu{}_\mu(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$.
    \item \emph{Coarse-grained macroscopic effective trace and legitimacy as a gravitational source}: Recently, Gordon~\cite{gordon2026prd} proposed a phenomenological \emph{unified effective trace ansatz} for self-similar Casimir hierarchies, arguing that when a cavity possesses an internal geometric length cutoff $\ell_*$ such that the dimensionless Casimir coefficient $\mathcal{C}(X)$ ($X \equiv \ln(d/\ell_*)$) runs log-periodically with plate separation $d$, the cavity-averaged macroscopic stress tensor acquires a non-vanishing \emph{integrated effective trace} $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}} = -(\hbar c/d^4)\mathcal{C}'(X)$. Crucially, however, simply contracting Eq.~\eqref{eq:semiclassical_einstein} to $R = -(8\pi G/c^4)T^\mu{}_\mu$ and substituting a cavity-integrated ansatz $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}}$ is \emph{not} equivalent to solving the local Einstein equations: first, contracting $G_{\mu\nu}$ discards the traceless Ricci and Weyl tensors that govern Newtonian potentials, tidal shears, gravitomagnetic frame dragging, and spin-2 waves; second, for conformally invariant fields ($\xi = 1/6$ and ideal electromagnetism), the pointwise local trace vanishes identically at every interior vacuum point $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^3 \setminus \partial\Omega$ ($\eta^{\mu\nu}\langle\hat{T}_{\mu\nu}(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{EM}} = 0$, so $R_{\mathrm{local}}(\mathbf{x}) = 0$ in open interior vacuum), meaning that $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}}$ resides physically in distributional boundary/wall interaction stresses on $\partial\Omega$ and defines a \emph{volume-averaged (coarse-grained) scalar curvature diagnostic} $R_{\mathrm{eff}} \equiv -(8\pi G/c^4)T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}}$ over $V_{\mathrm{cav}}$---or a long-wavelength monopole ($l=0$) source for the massive scalaron field $\varphi$ in quadratic $f(R)$ gravity ($\lambda_\varphi \gg L$) when acoustic support relaxation lags behind external modulation. Accordingly, following the full linearized field-equation spirit of Buoninfante \emph{et al.}~\cite{buoninfante2019epjc} (who solved linearized quadratic-gravity field equations for parallel Casimir plates), we reconstruct the full local tensor $\langle\hat{T}_{\mu\nu}(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$ throughout 3D space and solve the complete linearized tensor Einstein, Starobinsky $f(R)$, and gravito-electromagnetic PDEs rather than relying on the scalar trace contraction alone.
\end{enumerate}

Within linearized semiclassical gravity around flat spacetime, Eq.~\eqref{eq:brown_maclay} imposes three structural degeneracies on canonical planar Casimir gravitation:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \emph{Vanishing Ricci scalar and scalaron source}: Contracting Eq.~\eqref{eq:semiclassical_einstein} yields $R = -(8\pi G/c^4)\langle \hat{T}^\mu{}_\mu\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}} = 0$ across the planar gap, and constant $\mathcal{C} = -\pi^2/720$ gives $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}} = 0$. In quadratic $f(R) = R + \alpha R^2$ or scalar-tensor gravity~\cite{starobinsky1980,damour1992,defelice2010,berry2011,pourhassan2025epjc}, the extra scalar degree of freedom (scalaron) couples to the stress-energy trace and therefore decouples from planar conformal vacuum fluctuations.
    \item \emph{Algebraic locking of force, energy, and energy-condition violation}: Because $P_\perp = 3\rho_{\mathrm{vac}} c^2 < 0$, the planar force cannot vanish at finite separation without the vacuum energy also vanishing. Along the plate normal $k^\mu = (1,0,0,1)$, the Null Energy Condition (NEC) projection $T_{\mu\nu}k^\mu k^\nu = \rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + P_\perp = 4\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 < 0$ and Strong Energy Condition (SEC) combination $(T_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2}g_{\mu\nu}T)u^\mu u^\nu = \frac{1}{2}(\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + \sum_i P_i) = \rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 < 0$ are unconditionally negative, forcing purely defocusing Raychaudhuri geodesic perturbations~\cite{raychaudhuri1955,kontou2020}.
    \item \emph{In-plane Lorentz boost invariance and rotational silence}: Parallel to the plates, the vacuum enthalpy density vanishes identically, $\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + P_\parallel = 0$. Under rigid rotation of axisymmetric parallel disks relative to asymptotic inertial observers, the planar Casimir state acquires zero first-order laboratory momentum density $T^{0\phi}_{\mathrm{vac}} \propto v_\phi(\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + P_\parallel) = 0$ (leaving only higher-order radiative vacuum friction $\propto \Omega^3$ on polarizable media~\cite{manjavacas2010,maghrebi2012}), and therefore sources zero stationary gravitomagnetic Lense--Thirring field~\cite{ciufolini2004,mashhoon2007,costa2014grg,costa2021universe}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Prior Literature on Gravitational Casimir Physics, Dynamic Backgrounds, and Energy Inequalities}
Extensive prior research has examined Casimir cavities placed inside externally prescribed gravitational fields. Early Fermi- and Rindler-coordinate analyses~\cite{sorge2005,bimonte2006,bimonte2007prd} showed that in a weak uniform gravitational field $Y \equiv g/c^2$ ($g_{00} \simeq -(1 + 2Yz)$), proper-time redshifting modifies the local scalar energy density across separation $a$ as $\epsilon_{\mathrm{Cas}}(z) = -[\pi^2\hbar c/(1440 a^4)][1 + 2Y(z - a/2)]$ (shifting by factor $1 + 2Ya$ across elevation $a$ while leaving the symmetric spatial average unchanged to first order~\cite{sorge2005,lima2021jcap}), and revealed apparent orientation-dependent forces when integrating $\langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu}\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$ without mechanical support stresses. Fulling, Milton, Parashar, Romeo, Shajesh, and Wagner~\cite{fulling2007,milton2014fall} resolved this via variational principles, proving that covariant conservation $\nabla_\mu(T^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{vac}} + T^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{con}}) = 0$ causes spatial pressures to cancel in global mass integrals by the relativistic virial (Laue) theorem, yielding passive and active gravitational mass shift $E_C/c^2$ in compliance with the WEP. Crucially, Bekenstein~\cite{bekenstein2013prd} (via the subdominant trace energy condition) and Cerdonio and Rovelli~\cite{cerdonio2015fly,cerdonio2015ijmpd} proved that realistic plates and spring/mechanical supports always carry positive rest mass $M_{\mathrm{plate}} \gg |E_C|/c^2$, so total system mass $M_{\mathrm{tot}} = M_{\mathrm{plate}} + E_C/c^2 > 0$ is strictly positive and negative Casimir binding energy can never produce overall negative mass or gravitational levitation. Complementing the \emph{Archimedes} vacuum-weighing experiment~\cite{calloni2014,calloni2016nima}, Sorge~\cite{sorge2021buoyancy} applied the quasi-local Tolman and Komar mass formalisms to prove independently that a static Casimir cavity experiences a consistent WEP-compliant vacuum buoyancy shift $E_C/c^2$, while Sorge and Wilson~\cite{sorge2019,wilson2020prd} demonstrated that a cavity in free fall toward a Schwarzschild black hole acquires extra energy from dynamical Casimir particle creation alongside tidal corrections.

More recently, time-dependent, rotating, and curved backgrounds have been shown to alter intracavity quantum states significantly. In flat Minkowski spacetime with rotating boundaries, Chernodub~\cite{chernodub2013prd}, Schaden~\cite{schaden2012arxiv}, and Flachi and Edmonds~\cite{flachi2023prd} demonstrated that non-inertial corotating frames modify quantum vacuum eigenmodes and Casimir free energies when tangential speeds approach relativistic scales or interact with background fields, though at zero temperature inside the speed-of-light cylinder ($\Omega L/c \ll 1$) no perpetual energy transfer occurs between zero-point fluctuations and rigid rotation~\cite{schaden2012arxiv} and intrinsic centrifugal/Coriolis shifts enter at $\mathcal{O}(\Omega^2 L^2/c^2)$. In curved backgrounds, Sorge~\cite{sorge2023memory} demonstrated a \emph{gravitational memory of the Casimir effect}, wherein a transient spacetime pulse leaves a permanent shift in vacuum energy; de Oliveira, Moreira, and Céleri~\cite{deoliveira2026entropy} derived particle creation and parametric-amplification resonances in the dynamical Casimir effect (DCE) driven by passing gravitational waves; Sorge~\cite{sorge2014kerr} and Zhang~\cite{zhang2017plb} proved that stationary Kerr-spacetime metric frame dragging ($g_{0i}$) renormalizes Casimir energy; and Buoninfante \emph{et al.}~\cite{buoninfante2019epjc}, Pourhassan~\cite{pourhassan2025epjc}, and Garattini and Tzikas~\cite{garattini2025epjc} analyzed Casimir cavities and wormholes in quadratic and $f(R) = R + \alpha R^2$ gravity. Crucially, Refs.~\cite{sorge2014kerr,sorge2023memory,deoliveira2026entropy,zhang2017plb,buoninfante2019epjc} address how external metrics or modified propagators perturb a passive cavity, whereas Refs.~\cite{pourhassan2025epjc,garattini2025epjc} study exotic wormhole throats. Conversely, any negative Casimir energy interpretation is constrained by QEIs: Ford and Roman~\cite{ford1995,ford1997} and Fewster and collaborators~\cite{fewster1998,fewster2006jmp,fewster2012} proved that while a static Casimir vacuum violates unrestricted long-time averaged energy conditions, the difference-renormalized energy relative to empty Minkowski vacuum obeys smeared worldline bounds $\int_{-\infty}^\infty \langle \hat{T}_{00}(t)\rangle |f(t)|^2 \dd t \ge -C/\tau^4$ on sub-reflection causal diamonds not intersecting boundaries, preventing macroscopic causal pathologies while requiring positive boundary stress concentrations~\cite{deutsch1979,graham2003,kontou2020}.

\subsection{Fractal Casimir Precedents, Lapidus Boundary Spectral Theory, and Delineation of Novel Contributions}
In flat Minkowski spacetime without gravity, it is crucial to distinguish \emph{intrinsic fractal manifolds} or extra fractal compactified dimensions---where wave operators act on spaces of non-integer spectral or Hausdorff dimension~\cite{akkermans2010,calcagni2010,cheng2013ijtp}---from \emph{Euclidean domains in $\mathbb{R}^3$ bounded by prefractal manifolds $\partial\Omega$}, governed mathematically by Lapidus's fractal-drum spectral theorem~\cite{lapidus1991tams}, in which bulk fields obey standard $(3+1)$-dimensional relativistic wave equations while self-similar boundaries imprint anomalous Weyl boundary terms and DSI log-periodic spectral oscillations~\cite{sornette1998} analogous to Choptuik's critical gravitational collapse~\cite{choptuik1993}. For self-similar plate hierarchies, Shajesh \emph{et al.}~\cite{shajesh2016,shajesh2017} solved explicit scalar recursion models for Cantor plate stacks and Sierpinski triangles, finding recursive sign changes and well-behaved convergence plateaus as prefractal order $N \to \infty$, while Gordon~\cite{gordon2026prd} proposed a phenomenological one-dimensional unified effective trace formula combining fractal thermodynamics with a postulated running Casimir coefficient. \subsection{Planck--Casimir Dimensionless Units and Dimensional Analysis}
Rather than setting $\hbar = c = G = 1$ implicitly, we retain International System (SI) physical dimensions explicitly and verify dimensional homogeneity before mapping to dimensionless variables. Let $L$ denote the outer cavity radius ($[L] = \mathrm{m}$). Zero-temperature Casimir energy densities carry dimensions $[\langle\hat{T}_{\mu\nu}\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}] = [\hbar c / L^4] = \mathrm{J}\,\mathrm{m}^{-3}$, while Einstein's gravitational coupling carries $[8\pi G/c^4] = \mathrm{m}\,\mathrm{J}^{-1}$; their product has dimensions $[(8\pi G/c^4)\langle\hat{T}_{\mu\nu}\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}] = \mathrm{m}^{-2} = [G_{\mu\nu}] = [R]$ and defines the squared Planck length $\ell_P^2 = \hbar G / c^3 \approx 2.612 \times 10^{-70}\text{ m}^2$ and dimensionless Planck--cavity ratio $\epsilon_P \equiv \ell_P / L$. Similarly, in Starobinsky gravity $f(R) = R + \alpha R^2$, dimensional consistency of $f(R)$ requires $[\alpha] = [R^{-1}] = \mathrm{m}^2$ ($\alpha > 0$), so the scalaron inverse Compton wavelength $m_\varphi = 1/\sqrt{6\alpha}$ carries $[\mathrm{m}^{-1}]$ (corresponding to rest mass $M_\varphi = \hbar / (c\sqrt{6\alpha})$) and $\tilde{m}_\varphi \equiv m_\varphi L = L/\sqrt{6\alpha}$ is dimensionless. To prevent floating-point underflow while preserving exact physical scaling, we define dimensionless tilde quantities:
\begin{align}
\tilde{x}^\mu &= \frac{x^\mu}{L}, \qquad \tilde{\kappa} = \kappa L, \qquad \tilde{E}_C = \frac{L}{\hbar c} E_C, \qquad \tilde{F}_z = \frac{L^2}{\hbar c} F_z, \nonumber\\
\tilde{T}_{\mu\nu} &= \frac{L^4}{\hbar c} \langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu} \rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}, \qquad \tilde{R}_{\mu\nu} = \frac{L^4}{\ell_P^2} R_{\mu\nu}, \qquad \tilde{\bar{h}}_{\mu\nu} = \frac{L^2}{\ell_P^2} \bar{h}_{\mu\nu},
\label{eq:planck_units}
\end{align}
where $\bar{h}_{\mu\nu} \equiv h_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2}\eta_{\mu\nu}\eta^{\alpha\beta}h_{\alpha\beta}$ is the trace-reversed metric perturbation in Lorenz gauge $\partial^\mu \bar{h}_{\mu\nu} = 0$. In these units, the linearized semiclassical Einstein equation takes the parameter-free form
\begin{equation}
\tilde{\Box} \tilde{\bar{h}}_{\mu\nu}(\tilde{x}) = \left(-\frac{\partial^2}{\partial \tilde{x}_0^2} + \tilde{\nabla}^2\right)\tilde{\bar{h}}_{\mu\nu}(\tilde{x}) = -16\pi \, \tilde{T}_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{tot}}(\tilde{x}),
\label{eq:dimensionless_einstein}
\end{equation}
with local dimensionless Ricci scalar $\tilde{R}(\tilde{x}) = -8\pi \tilde{T}^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{tot}}(\tilde{x})$. Physical SI values at any reference length scale $L$ follow via $h_{\mu\nu} = \epsilon_P^2 \tilde{h}_{\mu\nu}$ and $R = (\epsilon_P^2/L^2)\tilde{R}$.

\subsection{Quantum Field Models, Exact Reduction of the Macroscopic Effective Trace, and Covariant Conservation}
\label{subsec:effective_trace_covariant}

To separate spin and local conformal properties, we evaluate three massless $T = 0\text{ K}$ quantum field models on the CPSC boundaries $\partial\Omega = \partial\Omega_{\mathrm{up}} \cup \partial\Omega_{\mathrm{low}}$:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \emph{Conformal massless real scalar ($\xi = 1/6$)}: Governed by the Callan--Coleman--Jackiw improved stress-energy tensor~\cite{callan1970,birrell1982},
    \begin{equation}
    \hat{T}_{\mu\nu}^{(\xi)} = \partial_\mu \hat{\phi}\,\partial_\nu \hat{\phi} - \frac{1}{2}\eta_{\mu\nu}(\partial\hat{\phi})^2 + \xi\bigl(\eta_{\mu\nu}\Box - \partial_\mu\partial_\nu\bigr)\hat{\phi}^2,
    \label{eq:scalar_tmunu}
    \end{equation}
    satisfying $\eta^{\mu\nu}\langle\hat{T}_{\mu\nu}^{(1/6)}(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}} = 0$ identically on shell ($\Box\hat{\phi}=0$) everywhere in interior vacuum $\mathbb{R}^3 \setminus \partial\Omega$.
    \item \emph{Minimally coupled massless real scalar ($\xi = 0$)}: Carries an intrinsic local bulk trace $\eta^{\mu\nu}\langle \hat{T}_{\mu\nu}^{(0)}(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}} = -\frac{1}{2}\Box\langle\hat{\phi}^2(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}} \neq 0$ whenever $\langle\hat{\phi}^2(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}$ varies spatially~\cite{deutsch1979,milton2016} (attaining normalized interior magnitude $\FcgConvInteriorScalarMinTraceRatio$ in our CPSC gap).
    \item \emph{Ideal $0\text{ K}$ PEC vector electromagnetism}: Governed by the renormalized Maxwell stress-energy tensor
    \begin{align}
    \langle \hat{T}_{00} \rangle_{\mathrm{EM}} &= \frac{1}{2}\left\langle \varepsilon_0 \mathbf{E}^2 + \mu_0 \mathbf{H}^2 \right\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}}, \nonumber\\
    \langle \hat{T}_{ij} \rangle_{\mathrm{EM}} &= \frac{1}{2}\delta_{ij}\left\langle \varepsilon_0 \mathbf{E}^2 + \mu_0 \mathbf{H}^2 \right\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}} - \left\langle \varepsilon_0 E_i E_j + \mu_0 H_i H_j \right\rangle_{\mathrm{ren}},
    \label{eq:maxwell_tmunu}
    \end{align}
    which is pointwise traceless ($\eta^{\mu\nu}\langle\hat{T}_{\mu\nu}(\mathbf{x})\rangle_{\mathrm{EM}} = 0$) at every interior point $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^3 \setminus \partial\Omega$ (verified numerically in our CPSC solver to relative residual $\FcgConvInteriorEmTraceNullResidual$).
\end{enumerate}

To formulate the three-dimensional macroscopic effective trace unambiguously and connect phenomenological scaling~\cite{gordon2026prd} to first-principles prefractal scattering, define the dimensionless logarithmic separation and radial variables
\begin{equation}
X \equiv \ln\frac{d}{\ell_*}, \qquad Y \equiv \ln\frac{R}{\ell_*},
\label{eq:log_scale_vars}
\end{equation}
at fixed physical prefractal cutoff length $\ell_* = \Delta r\,b^{-N}$ ($d = |z|$, annular area $A_{\mathrm{ann}} = \pi(r_{\mathrm{out}}^2 - r_{\mathrm{in}}^2) \propto R^2$). By dimensional analysis in $\hbar, c$, the exact zero-temperature Casimir interaction energy of a generation-$N$ prefractal cavity writes without loss of generality as
\begin{equation}
\frac{E_{C,N}(\theta, d, R; \ell_*)}{A_{\mathrm{ann}}} = \frac{\hbar c}{d^3}\,\mathcal{C}_N(\theta, X, Y),
\label{eq:dimensional_casimir_scaling}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{C}_{N,X} \equiv \partial\mathcal{C}_N/\partial X \equiv \dd\mathcal{C}_N/\dd(\ln d)|_{\theta,Y}$ and $\mathcal{C}_{N,Y} \equiv \partial\mathcal{C}_N/\partial Y \equiv \dd\mathcal{C}_N/\dd(\ln R)|_{\theta,X}$ denote explicit partial derivatives with respect to $X$ and $Y$ (written $\mathcal{C}'_N(X) \equiv \dd\mathcal{C}_N/\dd X$ when holding $R$ fixed). Defining the coarse-grained vacuum energy density $\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 \equiv E_{C,N} / V_{\mathrm{cav}}$ and virtual-work macroscopic cavity pressures over $V_{\mathrm{cav}} = A_{\mathrm{ann}} d$ at fixed $\ell_*$ gives
\begin{align}
\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 &= \frac{\hbar c}{d^4}\mathcal{C}_N, \nonumber\\
P_z &= -\frac{1}{A_{\mathrm{ann}}}\frac{\partial E_{C,N}}{\partial d}\Bigg|_{R,\ell_*} = \frac{\hbar c}{d^4}\bigl(3\mathcal{C}_N - \mathcal{C}_{N,X}\bigr), \nonumber\\
P_r + P_\phi &= -\frac{1}{d}\frac{R}{A_{\mathrm{ann}}}\frac{\partial E_{C,N}}{\partial R}\Bigg|_{d,\ell_*} = -\frac{\hbar c}{d^4}\bigl(2\mathcal{C}_N + \mathcal{C}_{N,Y}\bigr).
\label{eq:macroscopic_pressures_si}
\end{align}
Summing the mixed coarse-grained components $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}} \equiv -\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}c^2 + P_r + P_\phi + P_z$ cancels $3\mathcal{C}_N - \mathcal{C}_N - 2\mathcal{C}_N = 0$ identically, leaving in dimensional and dimensionless forms ($\tilde{d} = |\tilde{z}|$):
\begin{align}
T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}} &= -\frac{\hbar c}{d^4}\bigl(\mathcal{C}_{N,X} + \mathcal{C}_{N,Y}\bigr)\Big|_{\ell_*}, \nonumber\\
\tilde{T}^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}}(\theta, \tilde{z}) &= -\frac{1}{\tilde{V}_{\mathrm{cav}}}\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{cav}}\tilde{E}_{C,N} = -\frac{1}{|\tilde{z}|^4}\bigl(\mathcal{C}_{N,X} + \mathcal{C}_{N,Y}\bigr)\Big|_{\tilde{\ell}_*},
\label{eq:effective_trace_3d}
\end{align}
where $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{cav}} \equiv 1 + |\tilde{z}|\partial_{|\tilde{z}|}|_{\tilde{\ell}_*} + \tilde{R}\partial_{\tilde{R}}|_{\tilde{\ell}_*}$. In the 1D plate-stack limit ($\mathcal{C}_{N,Y} = 0$), Eq.~\eqref{eq:effective_trace_3d} reduces identically to $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}} = -(\hbar c/d^4)\mathcal{C}'_N(X)$~\cite{gordon2026prd}; for featureless plates ($\mathcal{C} = -\pi^2/720$), $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}} = 0$; and by Euler homogeneity, $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{cav}}\tilde{E}_{C,N} = -\tilde{\ell}_*\partial_{\tilde{\ell}_*}\tilde{E}_{C,N}$ reflects boundary work against the prefractal cutoff $\tilde{\ell}_*$. Moreover, whereas finite-temperature radiation on an intrinsic fractal of spectral dimension $d_s$ obeys Akkermans, Dunne, and Teplyaev's fractal Stefan--Boltzmann equation of state $p_{\mathrm{th}}V_s = U_{\mathrm{th}}/d_s$~\cite{akkermans2010}---yielding coarse-grained thermal trace $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{th}} = -\rho_{\mathrm{th}} + 3p_{\mathrm{th}} = \rho_{\mathrm{th}}(3/d_s - 1)$ per spectral volume $V_s$~\cite{gordon2026prd}---our CPSC operates strictly at $T = 0\text{ K}$ ($\rho_{\mathrm{th}} = 0$) in Euclidean $\mathbb{R}^3$ bulk~\cite{lapidus1991tams}, so $T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{th}} = 0$ identically.

Crucially, in Ref.~\cite{gordon2026prd}, postulating a log-periodic $\mathcal{C}(X)$ constituted a phenomenological ansatz rather than a microscopic derivation. Here we both derive $\mathcal{C}_N(X)$ analytically for a self-similar prefractal model and compute $\mathcal{C}_N$ from first principles via Eq.~\eqref{eq:bem_logdet}. Specifically, in a homothetic generation-$N$ Cantor hierarchy with characteristic stage depths $Z_m = Z_1 b^{-(m-1)}$ ($m = 1,\dots,N$, cutoff $\ell_* = Z_1 b^{-N}$, $X_0 \equiv \ln(d/Z_1) = X - N\ln b$), each self-similar stage contributes through a localized dimensionless Euclidean Yukawa scattering window $\Phi(\xi)$ ($\Phi(\xi) \to 0$ for $|\xi|\gg 1$), obeying the exact finite-$N$ recursion
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{C}_N(X_0) = \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{bg}} + \sum_{m=1}^{N} q^{m-1}\,\Phi\bigl(X_0 + (m-1)\ln b\bigr),
\label{eq:cantor_recursion_c}
\end{equation}
where $q$ is the inter-generation coupling weight. At fixed physical gap $d > 0$ in a coplanar Cantor cavity, sub-wavelength generations ($W_m \ll d$) are Yukawa-screened with $q < 1$ ($q_{\mathrm{area}} = 2/b = 2/3$ geometrically and $q_{\mathrm{evan}} = 2/b^2 = 2/9$ for evanescent dipole scattering), guaranteeing absolute convergence of both $\lim_{N\to\infty}\mathcal{C}_N(d)$ and $\lim_{N\to\infty}\mathcal{C}'_N(X)$. Conversely, probing homothetically across the scaling window $\ell_* \ll d \ll Z_1$ ($q = 1$ along the self-similar ray) and applying Poisson summation as $N$ grows yields the discrete-scale-invariant Fourier--Mellin expansion
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{C}_N(\theta, X) \simeq \sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty} \mathcal{C}_k(\theta) e^{i \omega_k X}, \qquad \omega_k = \frac{2\pi k}{\ln b}, \quad \mathcal{C}_k = \frac{\hat{\Phi}(\omega_k)}{\ln b},
\label{eq:dsi_fourier}
\end{equation}
establishing how discrete finite-$N$ modes transition smoothly into log-periodic running $\mathcal{C}'_N(X) \simeq \sum_{k\neq 0} i\omega_k \mathcal{C}_k e^{i\omega_k X}$ inside $\ell_* \ll d \ll Z_1$ while vanishing outside the prefractal window.

Finally, Lorenz-gauge Einstein consistency ($\partial^\mu \tilde{\Box}\tilde{\bar{h}}_{\mu\nu} = 0$) requires exact conservation of the \emph{total} stress-energy tensor:
\begin{equation}
\partial_\mu \tilde{T}^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{tot}} = \partial_\mu \Bigl( \tilde{T}^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{vac}} + \tilde{T}^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{con}} \Bigr) = 0,
\label{eq:covariant_conservation}
\end{equation}
where $\tilde{T}^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{con}}$ represents mechanical support stresses balancing Casimir forces $\tilde{F}_z(\theta)$ and torques $\tilde{\tau}_\theta(\theta) = -\partial_\theta \tilde{E}_C(\theta)$~\cite{fulling2007,milton2014fall,sorge2021buoyancy}, plus shaft power $\partial_{\tilde{t}}\tilde{E}_C = -\tilde{\Omega}\tilde{\tau}_\theta$. Throughout, we state three explicit physical idealizations: (i) boundaries and supports are modeled in the rigid, lossless limit ($Y_{\mathrm{rigid}} \to \infty$, elastic energy $\sim P^2/Y_{\mathrm{rigid}} \to 0$); (ii) rotation is strictly sub-relativistic inside the light cylinder ($\tilde{\Omega} = \Omega L/c \ll 1$), so non-inertial Unruh/corotating vacuum shifts~\cite{chernodub2013prd,schaden2012arxiv,flachi2023prd} are suppressed to $\mathcal{O}(\tilde{\Omega}^2)$; and (iii) while classical plate rest mass $M_{\mathrm{plate}}$ and moment of inertia $I_{\mathrm{plate}}\Omega$ dominate static monopole backgrounds in hardware, $C_6/C_4$ symmetry cancels the classical $l=2$ mass quadrupole identically in Phenomenon I while angular/radial differential subtraction isolates the vacuum backreaction in Phenomena II--V (Appendix~\ref{app:virial}).

\section{First-Principles Scattering, Voxel Correlator, and Convergence Verification}
\label{sec:methods}

To evaluate $\tilde{E}_C$, $\tilde{T}_{\mu\nu}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}})$, $\tilde{T}^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{eff}}$, and all induced gravitational perturbations without proximity-force approximations, we implement three coupled solvers verified by systematic convergence tests.

\subsection{3D Cyclic-Bloch Boundary-Element Scattering, Prefractal Regularization, and Scale-Derivative Engine}
\label{subsec:methods_bem}
Along the full imaginary frequency axis $\xi \in (-\infty, +\infty)$, the zero-temperature Casimir interaction energy reads $E_C = \frac{\hbar}{2}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{\dd\xi}{2\pi}\ln\det[\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{M}(|\xi|)]$; folding onto the positive Wick wavenumber axis $\tilde{\kappa} = \xi L / c \in (0, \infty)$ cancels the zero-point $\frac{1}{2}$ factor universally~\cite{reid2009,reid2013}:
\begin{equation}
\tilde{E}_C(\theta, \tilde{z}) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_0^\infty \dd\tilde{\kappa} \, \ln\det\bigl[\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{M}(\tilde{\kappa}; \theta, \tilde{z})\bigr],
\label{eq:bem_logdet}
\end{equation}
where polarization multiplicity is encoded directly in the dimension of $\mathbf{M}(\tilde{\kappa}) = \mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{UU}}^{-1}\mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{UL}}\mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{LL}}^{-1}\mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{LU}}$ ($N_p \times N_p$ for one real scalar field, reproducing $\tilde{\epsilon}_{\mathrm{Cas}}^{\mathrm{scalar}} = -\pi^2/(1440\tilde{d}^4)$ in the planar limit; $2N_p \times 2N_p$ for two-polarization PEC electromagnetism, reproducing $\tilde{\epsilon}_{\mathrm{Cas}}^{\mathrm{EM}} = -\pi^2/(720\tilde{d}^4)$).

A key theoretical requirement in prefractal geometries is rigorous ultraviolet regularization and renormalization of edge and corner divergences. In single-domain zeta-function or Seeley--DeWitt heat-kernel expansions~\cite{vassilevich2003physrep,bordag2009}, $\mathrm{Tr}(e^{-s\Delta})$ generates bulk volume ($s^{-2}$), Weyl surface area ($s^{-3/2}$), $90^\circ$ dihedral slot-edge ($s^{-1} L_{\mathrm{edge}} \propto 2^N$), and corner ($s^{-1/2}$) divergences alongside finite prefractal crossover boundary counterterms~\cite{lapidus1991tams,shajesh2016,gordon2026prd}. Because our CPSC consists of two strictly disjoint rigid assemblies ($\partial\Omega_{\mathrm{U}} \cap \partial\Omega_{\mathrm{L}} = \emptyset$ with minimum clearance $\tilde{d}_{\min} > 0$), every local bulk, surface, $2^N$-edge, and corner divergence resides inside the isolated self-energies of $\partial\Omega_{\mathrm{U}}$ or $\partial\Omega_{\mathrm{L}}$ alone. Factoring the coupled scattering determinant as $\det\mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{U+L}}/(\det\mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{UU}}\det\mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{LL}}) = \det[\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{M}(\tilde{\kappa})]$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:bem_logdet} cancels all bulk, surface, prefractal edge, and corner counterterms identically prior to $\tilde{\kappa}$-integration, leaving $\mathbf{M}(\tilde{\kappa})$ trace-class and exponentially convergent ($\|\mathbf{M}(\tilde{\kappa})\| \sim e^{-2\tilde{\kappa}\tilde{d}_{\min}}$ as $\tilde{\kappa} \to \infty$) without ad hoc counterterms. Boundary surfaces are meshed into $N_p$ quadrilateral/annular panels $\{\mathcal{P}_a\}_{a=1}^{N_p}$ with piecewise-constant basis functions; integrable panel self-interactions are evaluated analytically via equal-area disk regularization $\tilde{\sigma}_a = \sqrt{\tilde{A}_a/(16\pi)}$:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \emph{Scalar Dirichlet kernel}: Single-layer Euclidean Yukawa kernel $G_0(\tilde{\mathbf{r}}, \tilde{\mathbf{r}}'; \tilde{\kappa}) = \exp(-\tilde{\kappa}|\tilde{\mathbf{r}}-\tilde{\mathbf{r}}'|)/(4\pi|\tilde{\mathbf{r}}-\tilde{\mathbf{r}}'|)$ ($N_p$ degrees of freedom).
    \item \emph{PEC vector-dyadic kernel}: Electric-field integral equation (EFIE) projecting two tangential surface-current basis vectors $(\hat{\mathbf{t}}_{a,1}, \hat{\mathbf{t}}_{a,2})$ per panel onto the Euclidean dyadic Green's tensor $\mathbf{G}(\tilde{\mathbf{r}}, \tilde{\mathbf{r}}'; \tilde{\kappa}) = \bigl(\mathbf{I} - \tilde{\kappa}^{-2}\tilde{\nabla}\tilde{\nabla}\bigr) G_0(\tilde{\mathbf{r}}, \tilde{\mathbf{r}}'; \tilde{\kappa})$ ($2N_p$ degrees of freedom).
\end{itemize}
For constellations with cyclic greatest common divisor $M_g = \gcd(M_{\mathrm{up}}, M_{\mathrm{low}}) > 1$ ($M_g = 4$ for $(4,4)$ and $M_g = 2$ for $(6,4)$), discrete Fourier projection into $M_g$ decoupled Bloch blocks $\mathbf{M}^{(\ell)}(\tilde{\kappa}) = \sum_{q=0}^{M_g-1} e^{2\pi i \ell q / M_g} \mathbf{M}_{0,q}(\tilde{\kappa})$ accelerates LU factorization by $M_g^3$ without approximation (verified against full-ring dense LU to $<10^{-11}$ relative error). Integrals over $\tilde{\kappa}$ use log-mapped Gauss--Legendre quadrature on $\tilde{\kappa} \in [10^{-2}, 1.5\times 10^2]$, and parametric derivatives ($\tilde{F}_z$, $\tilde{\tau}_\theta$, $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{cav}}\tilde{E}_C$) use centered finite differences of logarithmic step $\Delta\ln d = 0.015$.

\subsection{Four-Pass Inclusion--Exclusion 3D Euclidean Correlator Voxel Mapping}
\label{subsec:methods_voxel}
To reconstruct the spatially resolved renormalized stress tensor $\tilde{T}_{\mu\nu}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}; \theta)$ at interior vacuum points $\tilde{\mathbf{x}} \in \Omega_{\mathrm{grid}} \subset \mathbb{R}^3 \setminus \partial\Omega$ (masked outside solid conductor volumes by safety clearance $\tilde{\delta}_{\mathrm{wall}} = 0.008$, corresponding physically to finite plasma skin-depth regularization~\cite{deutsch1979,graham2003}), we evaluate the scattered Euclidean Green's correlator $\Delta G(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}, \tilde{\mathbf{x}}'; \tilde{\kappa}) \equiv G^{\mathrm{full}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}, \tilde{\mathbf{x}}'; \tilde{\kappa}) - G_0(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}, \tilde{\mathbf{x}}'; \tilde{\kappa})$. Subtracting free-space coincidence divergences ($G_0$) directly and cancelling single-body edge/surface stresses via four-pass inclusion--exclusion yields
\begin{equation}
\Delta \tilde{T}_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{mut}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}; \theta) = \tilde{T}_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{U+L}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}; \theta) - \tilde{T}_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{U}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}; \theta) - \tilde{T}_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{L}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}) + \tilde{T}_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{vac}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}),
\label{eq:four_pass_correlator}
\end{equation}
alongside the boundary-renormalized total interior tensor $\tilde{T}_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{ren}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}; \theta) = \tilde{T}_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{U+L}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}; \theta) - \tilde{T}_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{vac}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}})$, evaluated from the LU-factorized scattering inverse $\mathbf{\Gamma}^{-1}(\tilde{\kappa})$:
\begin{equation}
\Delta G_{\mathrm{ren}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}, \tilde{\mathbf{x}}'; \tilde{\kappa}) = -\sum_{a,b} g_0(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}, \mathcal{P}_a; \tilde{\kappa}) \bigl[\mathbf{\Gamma}^{-1}(\tilde{\kappa})\bigr]_{ab} g_0(\mathcal{P}_b, \tilde{\mathbf{x}}'; \tilde{\kappa}).
\label{eq:scattered_greens_pointwise}
\end{equation}
Analytical spatial derivatives $\partial_i \partial'_j \Delta G_{\mathrm{ren}}|_{\tilde{\mathbf{x}}'=\tilde{\mathbf{x}}}$ and $\tilde{\kappa}^2 \Delta G_{\mathrm{ren}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}, \tilde{\mathbf{x}}; \tilde{\kappa})$ assemble the scalar~\eqref{eq:scalar_tmunu} and dyadic electromagnetic~\eqref{eq:maxwell_tmunu} stress tensors at each voxel.

\subsection{3D Spectral Linearized Einstein, Scalaron, and Gravitomagnetic PDE Solvers}
\label{subsec:methods_pde}
All linear-algebra, quadrature, Fast Fourier Transform (FFT), and root-finding routines are implemented in double-precision Python using \textsc{NumPy}~\cite{harris2020numpy} and \textsc{SciPy}~\cite{virtanen2020scipy}. From $\tilde{T}_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{tot}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}, \tilde{t})$, gravitational observables are computed across three sectors:

\section{Conclusion}

We have formulated a self-contained semiclassical field-theoretic and numerical framework for prefractal Casimir gravitation at zero temperature, uniting three-dimensional boundary-element scattering with linearized Einstein, Starobinsky $f(R)$, and gravito-electromagnetic theory. Second, while real-spacetime amplitudes are suppressed by $\epsilon_P^2 \sim 10^{-58}$ at micrometer length scales, mapping the Vernier selection rules and broken-boost enthalpy currents onto metamaterial and superfluid analog-gravity systems provides a concrete avenue for future experimental exploration.

\section{Covariant Virial Identity and Conservation of the Total Quadrupole Tensor}
\label{app:virial}

In linearized general relativity around Minkowski spacetime, Lorenz gauge $\partial^\mu \bar{h}_{\mu\nu} = 0$ requires $\partial_\mu T^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{tot}} = 0$:
\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial \tilde{T}^{00}_{\mathrm{tot}}}{\partial \tilde{t}} + \partial_j \tilde{T}^{j0}_{\mathrm{tot}} = 0, \qquad \frac{\partial \tilde{T}^{0i}_{\mathrm{tot}}}{\partial \tilde{t}} + \partial_j \tilde{T}^{ji}_{\mathrm{tot}} = 0.
\label{eq:app_continuity}
\end{equation}
Multiplying the energy continuity equation by $\tilde{x}^i\tilde{x}^j$, integrating over a ball $\mathcal{B}_R$ enclosing the isolated cavity-plus-support assembly ($\tilde{T}^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{tot}} = 0$ on $\partial\mathcal{B}_R$), and integrating by parts twice using momentum continuity yields the relativistic second-moment virial theorem:
\begin{equation}
\frac{\dd^2}{\dd\tilde{t}^2} \int_{\mathcal{B}_R} \tilde{T}^{00}_{\mathrm{tot}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}, \tilde{t}) \, \tilde{x}^i \tilde{x}^j \, \dd^3\tilde{x} = 2 \int_{\mathcal{B}_R} \tilde{T}^{ij}_{\mathrm{tot}}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}, \tilde{t}) \, \dd^3\tilde{x}.
\label{eq:app_virial}
\end{equation}
In the ideal rigid-support limit ($Y_{\mathrm{rigid}} \to \infty$), elastic displacements scale as $\delta\tilde{u} \sim \tilde{P}/Y_{\mathrm{rigid}} \to 0$, so elastic strain energy vanishes ($\tilde{T}^{00}_{\mathrm{con,elas}} \sim \tilde{P}^2/Y_{\mathrm{rigid}} \to 0$) while constraint stresses $\tilde{T}^{ij}_{\mathrm{con}} \sim \mathcal{O}(\tilde{P})$ satisfy $\partial_j \tilde{T}^{ji}_{\mathrm{con}} = -\partial_j \tilde{T}^{ji}_{\mathrm{vac}} - \partial_{\tilde{t}}\tilde{T}^{0i}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ at boundary walls. For static configurations ($\partial_{\tilde{t}} = 0$), Eq.~\eqref{eq:app_virial} gives $\int \tilde{T}^{ij}_{\mathrm{con}}\dd^3\tilde{x} = -\int \tilde{T}^{ij}_{\mathrm{vac}}\dd^3\tilde{x}$ (Laue's theorem), ensuring $\Delta\tilde{M}_{\mathrm{Komar}}(\infty) = \tilde{E}_C$~\cite{fulling2007,sorge2021buoyancy}. During steady rotation $\theta(\tilde{t}) = \tilde{\Omega}\tilde{t}$ of the $(6,4)$ Vernier cavity, $\tilde{T}^{00}_{\mathrm{vac}}$ carries the $m=2$ wave~\eqref{eq:vernier_vacuum_wave} whereas rigid $C_6$ and $C_4$ mass densities carry zero $m=2$ harmonic; by Eq.~\eqref{eq:app_virial}, $\int (\tilde{T}^{ij}_{\mathrm{vac}} + \tilde{T}^{ij}_{\mathrm{con}})^{\mathrm{TF}}\dd^3\tilde{x} = \frac{1}{2}\ddot{\tilde{\mathcal{Q}}}_{ij}^{\mathrm{vac}}$, verified numerically to relative error $\FcgGwVirialConservationRelErr$.

\section{Scattering-Operator Derivation of the Vernier Multipole Selection Rule and GW Reciprocity}
\label{app:vernier}

Let $\mathcal{U}(\alpha)$ denote the spatial rotation operator about the $z$ axis by angle $\alpha$. For an upper boundary $\partial\Omega_{\mathrm{U}}(\theta)$ with discrete $C_{M_{\mathrm{up}}}$ symmetry rotated by $\theta$ and a stationary lower boundary $\partial\Omega_{\mathrm{L}}$ with $C_{M_{\mathrm{low}}}$ symmetry, the single-body Euclidean scattering matrices satisfy $\mathcal{U}(2\pi/M_{\mathrm{low}})\mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{LL}}\mathcal{U}^{-1}(2\pi/M_{\mathrm{low}}) = \mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{LL}}$ and $\mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{UU}}(\theta) = \mathcal{U}(\theta)\mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{UU}}(0)\mathcal{U}^{-1}(\theta)$ with $\mathcal{U}(2\pi/M_{\mathrm{up}})\mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{UU}}(0)\mathcal{U}^{-1}(2\pi/M_{\mathrm{up}}) = \mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{UU}}(0)$. Expanding the off-diagonal propagators in cylindrical azimuthal harmonics $e^{i\ell\phi}$ ($\ell \in \mathbb{Z}$), $\mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{UU}}^{-1}(\theta)$ couples $\ell \to \ell + p M_{\mathrm{up}}$ with phase $e^{-i p M_{\mathrm{up}}\theta}$ ($p \in \mathbb{Z}$), whereas $\mathbf{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{LL}}^{-1}$ couples $\ell' \to \ell' - q M_{\mathrm{low}}$ ($q \in \mathbb{Z}$). Inserting this Neumann/Born expansion of $\mathbf{\Gamma}^{-1}(\tilde{\kappa}; \theta)$ into the coincidence Green's correlator~\eqref{eq:scattered_greens_pointwise} shows that every term in the renormalized vacuum energy density takes the exact double-Bloch series form
\begin{align}
\Delta\tilde{T}_{00}^{\mathrm{vac}}(\tilde{r}, \phi, \tilde{z}; \theta) &= \sum_{p=-\infty}^{\infty} \sum_{q=-\infty}^{\infty} \mathcal{A}_{p,q}(\tilde{r}, \tilde{z}) \nonumber\\
&\quad \times e^{i(p M_{\mathrm{up}} - q M_{\mathrm{low}})\phi - i p M_{\mathrm{up}}\theta}.
\label{eq:app_vernier_bloch}
\end{align}
Projecting onto radiative spin-$s$ spherical/cylindrical multipoles $e^{\mp i s \phi}$ ($s = 2$ for spin-2 quadrupole radiation) enforces the exact selection rule
\begin{equation}
p M_{\mathrm{up}} - q M_{\mathrm{low}} = \pm s, \qquad \omega_{\mathrm{rad}} = |p| M_{\mathrm{up}} \Omega.
\label{eq:app_vernier_selection}
\end{equation}
For $(M_{\mathrm{up}}, M_{\mathrm{low}}) = (6, 4)$ and $s = 2$, the minimal non-zero integers $(p, q) = (\pm 1, \pm 1)$ satisfy $6(\pm 1) - 4(\pm 1) = \pm 2$ and radiate at $\omega_{\mathrm{GW}} = 6\Omega$, whereas any non-interacting rigid body ($q = 0$ or $p = 0$) requires $6p = \pm 2$ or $-4q = \pm 2$, which has no integer solution. Conversely, if the $(6,4)$ cavity rotates inside an incoming circularly polarized plane gravitational wave $h_+^{\mathrm{ext}}(t) = h_0\cos(\omega_{\mathrm{GW}} t)$, the linear interaction Hamiltonian $H_{\mathrm{int}}(t) = -\frac{1}{2}\int h_{ij}^{\mathrm{ext}}(t) T^{ij}_{\mathrm{tot}}(\mathbf{x}, t)\dd^3x = -\frac{1}{4}\ddot{h}_{ij}^{\mathrm{ext}}(t)\mathcal{Q}_{ij}^{\mathrm{vac}}(t)$ yields a secular phase-locked energy transfer (and permanent memory shift in the sense of Sorge~\cite{sorge2023memory}) selectively when $\omega_{\mathrm{GW}} = 6\Omega$, while reducing in the high-frequency non-adiabatic limit ($\omega_{\mathrm{GW}} \to 2\omega_{\mathrm{cav}}$) to the parametric dynamical Casimir resonance derived by de Oliveira \emph{et al.}~\cite{deoliveira2026entropy}.
