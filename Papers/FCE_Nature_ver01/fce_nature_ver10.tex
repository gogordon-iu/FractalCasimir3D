\title[Vacuum Casimir forces in fractal geometries]{Amplification and switching of vacuum Casimir forces in fractal geometries}

\author*[1]{\fnm{Goren} \sur{Gordon}}\email{gogordon@iu.edu}

% Nature Flagship Abstract (strictly <= 200 words; placed before \maketitle in sn-jnl.cls)
\abstract{%
The quantum fluctuation-induced Casimir force between neutral bodies in vacuum is ordinarily attractive, governed by reflection symmetry and parallel-plate surface overlap. Sidewall fringing fields between interdigitated ridges and slots can reverse this sign at zero projected overlap, yet single ridges generate sub-piconewton forces, uniform gratings suffer mutual evanescent screening, and unreinforced nanoscale ribs risk lateral pull-in or mid-stroke snap-in. Here we introduce a three-dimensional homothetic Cantor rotary-axial switch (\texttt{H3-Cantor}) that couples radial middle-third subdivision with generation-scaled azimuthal lobe trains, axial staging, and suspended stator micro-bridges in vacuum. Across matched boundary-element, curved-beam finite-element, and cleanroom Monte Carlo benchmarks against five area- and gap-matched non-fractal controls, \texttt{H3-Cantor} delivers $+\roundtwo{\HthreeSymFzOpPn}\text{ pN}$ open-slot repulsion over a $\HthreeSymSpanNmDisp{}\text{ nm}$ sign-safe stroke, switches to $\roundtwo{\HthreeSymFzEngagedPn}\text{ pN}$ attraction upon rotation over stepped landing pads, elevates stator rib buckling safety above $10^7$, and achieves $\roundone{\HthreeSymYieldPct}\%$ fabrication yield. Orthogonal $2^3$ factorial, recursion-depth, and bridge ablations isolate radial--azimuthal force synergy from axial stroke stabilization. Finally, on a shared unit cell, first-principles Rao--Wilton--Glisson boundary-element and four-pass finite-difference time-domain solvers converge quantitatively to the same piconewton Casimir forces across repulsive and attractive states for both ideal conductors and dispersive gold.}

\maketitle

The Casimir effect---the macroscopic mechanical force generated by boundary-modified quantum electromagnetic fluctuations\cite{casimir1948,casimirpolder1948,bordag2009}---links quantum electrodynamics to the three-dimensional (3D) geometry of matter at sub-micrometre scales\cite{rodriguez2011}. In ordinary vacuum cavities ($\varepsilon_{\mathrm{bg}}=1.0$), both the sign and the spatial range of this interaction are tightly constrained. By the Kenneth--Klich theorem\cite{kennethklich2006}, mirror-symmetric bodies composed of non-magnetic conductors or dielectrics in vacuum attract at all separations, while under the proximity-force approximation (PFA) facing surfaces experience strong normal attraction proportional to their projected overlap area ($F_{z,\mathrm{face}}^{\mathrm{PFA}} \propto -A_{\mathrm{overlap}}/d^4$). Although repulsive Casimir--Lifshitz forces have been realized through high-index liquid immersion\cite{munday2009}, and vacuum nanomechanics has expanded to Casimir torques\cite{somers2018}, self-assembled polaritonic microcavities\cite{munkhbat2021}, non-reciprocal heat transfer\cite{Xu2022}, magnetic-field tuning\cite{Zhang2024}, and nonlinear superconducting resonators\cite{casimir_drum2025,dejong2026}, amplifying and reversibly switching repulsive Casimir forces in dry vacuum without viscous damping or electrostatic bias remains a central challenge\cite{rodriguez2011}.

Two seemingly distinct geometric principles offer complementary routes around parallel-plate attraction. First, in quantum fractals\cite{kempkes2019,xu2021} and self-similar plate hierarchies\cite{akkermans2010,shajesh2016,shajesh2017}, discrete scale invariance with scaling ratio $b>1$ replaces continuous scale covariance by a hierarchy of length scales $\ell_m = \ell_1 b^{-(m-1)}$ ($m=1,\dots,N$). Within an effective macroscopic trace framework for self-similar boundaries\cite{milton2004,milton2016,gordon2026prd}, discrete scale symmetry is parameterized by a logarithmically running Casimir coefficient $C(d_s,\ln(d/\ell_*))$ whose Fourier series in $\ln(d/\ell_*)$ carries harmonic frequencies $\omega_k = 2\pi k/\ln b$. Yet in 3D Euclidean cavities with facing surfaces ($A_{\mathrm{overlap}}>0$), Weyl--Lapidus spectral asymptotics\cite{lapidus1991} dictate that bulk volume and parallel-area terms overwhelm subleading perimeter and multiscale scattering contributions. Second, Levin et al.\cite{levin2010} showed that reflection symmetry in vacuum can be broken geometrically when a narrow metallic ridge sits centred above an open slot aperture ($A_{\mathrm{overlap}}=0$): saddle-point fringing fields linking the ridge to the slot sidewalls pull the ridge toward the slot mid-plane, producing an axial repulsive force ($+F_z>0$) accompanied by transverse Earnshaw instability that is stabilized by an elastic suspension\cite{levin2010,rodriguez2011}. Scaling this zero-overlap mechanism into a practical 3D actuator, however, confronts four coupled physical bottlenecks: a single ridge yields only sub-piconewton force; dense uniform gratings suffer mutual evanescent screening across narrow ribs while risking catastrophic lateral rib buckling; single-depth slots produce a narrow vertical repulsive window prone to mid-stroke snap-in; and sharp-cornered boundary discretizations have lacked quantitative cross-verification between independent first-principles 3D time- and frequency-domain Maxwell solvers.

Here we resolve all four bottlenecks simultaneously through a 3D homothetic Cantor rotary-axial Casimir switch (\texttt{H3-Cantor}) in vacuum ($\varepsilon_{\mathrm{bg}}=1.0$; Fig.~\ref{fig:h3_hero} and Extended Data Fig.~\ref{fig:blender_atlas}). By embedding a third-generation ($N=3$, base $b=3$) radial Cantor slot-tooth hierarchy into a $C_4$-symmetric annular cavity and scaling azimuthal lobe trains, axial stage elevations, and suspended stator rim micro-bridges homothetically across generations, \texttt{H3-Cantor} eliminates projected face overlap in its disengaged state ($\theta_{\mathrm{open}}=3^\circ$) to generate multi-piconewton repulsion across an extended multi-stage vertical stroke, while rotating by $45^\circ$ over stepped stator landing pads ($\theta_{\mathrm{eng}}=48^\circ$) restores parallel-plate overlap and switches the axial force to multi-piconewton attraction at zero electrical bias. We establish its nanomechanical robustness against five area- and gap-matched non-fractal controls (Fig.~\ref{fig:robustness} and Table~\ref{tab:unified_fractal_robustness}), decompose its coordinate mechanisms via orthogonal $2^3$ factorial, recursion-depth, and micro-bridge ablations (Fig.~\ref{fig:ablations} and Table~\ref{tab:h3_mechanistic_ablations}), and prove on a shared single-cell benchmark that exact 3D Rao--Wilton--Glisson (RWG) boundary-element\cite{rao1982,reid2013pra} and four-pass 3D finite-difference time-domain (FDTD)\cite{rodriguez2009pra,oskooi2010} solvers converge quantitatively to the same piconewton Casimir forces across both kinematic states and both ideal and dispersive metallic models (Fig.~\ref{fig:convergence} and Table~\ref{tab:single-setup-convergence}).

\section*{Homothetic 3D Cantor rotary switch}
\label{sec:architecture}

To motivate coupling radial, azimuthal, and axial scales by a common homothetic ratio $b=3$, consider the effective trace identity for a renormalized self-similar vacuum cavity of characteristic separation $d$ and $N$-th generation cutoff $\ell_* = L b^{-N}$\cite{gordon2026prd}:
\begin{equation}
T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{vac}} = -\rho_{\mathrm{vac}} + 2P_\parallel + P_\perp = -\frac{\hbar c}{d^4}\,\partial_{\ln d} C\!\left(d_s, \ln\frac{d}{\ell_*}\right),
\label{eq:trace_identity}
\end{equation}
where $\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}$ is the renormalized Casimir energy density and $P_\parallel, P_\perp$ denote transverse and normal pressures. In a 1D transfer-matrix hierarchy $\mathcal{C}_N$ of scale ratio $b$, recursive block partitioning $\Delta e_{\mathcal{C}_N}(d) = 2\Delta e_{\mathcal{C}_{N-1}}(d/b) + \Delta e_{\mathrm{cross}}(d;\ell_*)$ motivates log-spaced characteristic wavenumbers $\kappa_m \propto b^{m-1}$ ($\Delta\ln\kappa = \ln b$). Translating this discrete scale-covering principle into a physical 3D electromagnetic switch requires first extinguishing the leading $A_{\mathrm{overlap}}/d^4$ parallel-plate attraction ($A_{\mathrm{overlap}}=0$), and then scaling all three cylindrical coordinates $(r,\theta,z)$ concertedly so that coarse, medium, and fine tracks neither screen one another evanescently nor collapse their vertical stroke windows.

Along the radial coordinate ($I_r=1$), seven concentric tracks follow the symmetric third-generation ($N=3$, $b=3$) Cantor sequence $m_k \in (3,2,3,1,3,2,3)$ with homothetic scale factor $s_m = 3^{3-m} \in \{9,3,1\}$ for $m \in \{1,2,3\}$: slot widths $W_m = 90\ \mathrm{nm}\cdot s_m \in \{810, 270, 90\}\ \mathrm{nm}$ house central rotor teeth of width $w_m = 0.40\,W_m \in \{324, 108, 36\}\ \mathrm{nm}$ separated from copper stator ribs ($b_{\mathrm{rib}} \in [97.5, 165.0]\ \mathrm{nm}$) by radial clearances $g_{r,m} = 0.30\,W_m \in \{243, 81, 27\}\ \mathrm{nm}$ ($g_{\min} = \RobGminNmDisp{}\ \mathrm{nm}$). Along the azimuthal coordinate ($I_\theta=1$), each track is subdivided into $n_{\mathrm{lobe},m} \in \{2,4,8\}$ rotor lobes ($42$ lobes per sector, $A_{\mathrm{tooth}} = \RobAtoothUmtwoDisp{}\ \mu\mathrm{m}^2$ across four sectors) of homothetic chord length $c_m = 81\ \mathrm{nm}\cdot s_m \in \{729, 243, 81\}\ \mathrm{nm}$. Along the axial coordinate ($I_z=1$), stage mid-planes step homothetically as $z_{\mathrm{stage},m} = -40\ \mathrm{nm}\cdot s_m \in \{-360, -120, -40\}\ \mathrm{nm}$ with vertical heights $h_m = 0.70\,|z_{\mathrm{stage},m}| \in \{252, 84, 28\}\ \mathrm{nm}$ and stepped landing pads maintaining a uniform $27\ \mathrm{nm}$ engaged gap at the nominal operating elevation $z_{\mathrm{op}} = 58\ \mathrm{nm}$. Crucially, within the inter-lobe angular gaps of every open slot, emerald suspended stator rim micro-bridges (thicknesses $\{117, 39, 13\}\ \mathrm{nm}$, placed at the upper rim for $m=3$ and lower rims for $m=1,2$ so they remain axially disjoint from rotating rotor lobes across all $\theta \in [0^\circ, 90^\circ)$) tie opposing stator ribs together, clamping the maximum unsupported rib arc to $9.10^\circ$. We evaluate both the symmetric phase-aligned \texttt{H3-Cantor} ($\Delta\phi_m = 0^\circ$) and a Vernier phase-staggered variant (\texttt{H3-Cantor-Vernier}, $\Delta\phi_m \in \{0^\circ, +1.75^\circ, -1.15^\circ\}$; Methods).

\section*{Matched controls and lab robustness}
\label{sec:robustness}

To test whether three-dimensional homothetic Cantor organization confers a genuine physical advantage beyond fine minimum clearance $g_{\min}$ or active tooth area $A_{\mathrm{tooth}}$, we benchmark \texttt{H3-Cantor} and \texttt{H3-Cantor-Vernier} against five strictly matched non-fractal controls across the identical annular footprint $R \in [3.50, 6.20]\ \mu\mathrm{m}$, active tooth area $A_{\mathrm{tooth}} = \RobAtoothUmtwoDisp{}\ \mu\mathrm{m}^2$, room-temperature ($T=300\ \mathrm{K}$) Drude--Lorentz gold dielectric response\cite{rakic1998}, and unified $(2,2,2,9)$ 3D Vector-Dyadic BEM panel topology ($\HthreeMeshTtwoNr{}$ rotor and $\HthreeMeshTtwoNs{}$ stator panels per sector across $\RobNtotalBemTasks{}$ total 3D BEM evaluations; Extended Data Fig.~\ref{fig:blender_atlas} and Methods): (1) \texttt{Ctrl-KnifeEdge-g27}, packing $19$ coplanar minimum-gap ($g_r = 27\ \mathrm{nm}$) tracks separated by unbridged narrow ribs; (2) \texttt{Ctrl-Uni-7Trk}, a uniform 7-track single-stage grating; (3) \texttt{Ctrl-Lin-3Stage}, replacing geometric $b=3$ scaling with arithmetic 3-stage spacing; (4) \texttt{Ctrl-Mono-3Stage}, preserving the exact Cantor multiset of widths and heights in monotonic radial order $(3,3,3,3,2,2,1)$ without micro-bridges; and (5) \texttt{Ctrl-Shuf-OutPhase}, misaligning radial slot widths against $(z,\theta)$ stage assignments. Automated 3D Blender mesh audits verify zero interpenetration, active-area error $\le \roundthree{\HthreeBlenderMaxAreaErrUmtwo}\ \mu\mathrm{m}^2$, and minimum clearance $\ge \HthreeBlenderMinClearanceNm{}\ \mathrm{nm}$ across all $\HthreeBlenderPassedAudits{}/\HthreeBlenderTotalConfigs{}$ primary architectures (Extended Data Fig.~\ref{fig:blender_atlas}).

\section*{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Our results demonstrate that three-dimensional homothetic prefractal organization provides an effective architectural principle for engineering quantum vacuum forces when paired with zero-overlap slot-tooth interdigitation. Eliminating horizontal face overlap ($A_{\mathrm{overlap}}=0$) removes the leading attractive Weyl/PFA area term that ordinarily masks boundary hierarchy effects in 3D cavities\cite{kennethklich2006,lapidus1991}, exposing a perimeter- and sidewall-dominated scattering regime\cite{levin2010}. Within that window, radial Cantor scaling and generation-scaled azimuthal lobe segmentation act synergistically ($\mathcal{I}_{r\theta} = \AblYatesOpenIrth{}\ \mathrm{pN}$) to pack high sidewall-proximity density without the evanescent self-screening or catastrophic rib-buckling fragility of dense uniform gratings, while homothetic axial staging strings three generation-resolved repulsive basins into a continuous $\HthreeSymSpanNmDisp{}\ \mathrm{nm}$ sign-safe stroke and suspended rim micro-bridges stiffen stator ribs by more than two orders of magnitude ($\AblBridgeSfGainRatio{}\times$). Quantitative convergence between first-principles RWG BEM and four-pass 3D FDTD across both repulsive and attractive states establishes a rigorous computational foundation for cleanroom-fabricable vacuum Casimir switches, stiction-free nanoelectromechanical clutches, and zero-bias quantum-fluctuation sensors\cite{safronova2018,lodahl2015}.

\subsection*{1D self-similar transfer-matrix heuristic and spectral/stroke analysis}
In the motivating 1D scalar hierarchy $\mathcal{C}_N$ of scale factor $b=3$\cite{milton2004,gordon2026prd}, each prefractal generation subdivides intervals by $1/b$, motivating generation-indexed characteristic imaginary wavenumbers $\kappa_m \sim g_{r,m}^{-1} \propto b^{m-1}$. In 3D BEM, we extract the generation-resolved coupling spectrum $w_m(\kappa)$ ($m \in \{1,2,3\}$) across a hybrid logarithmic grid of $273$ total $(m,\kappa)$ samples by isolating radial-sidewall panels belonging to generation $m$, fitting local quadratic peaks $\ln\hat{\kappa}_m$ around each sub-band maximum, and comparing exact $\ln 3$-spaced, compressed-periodic, and unrestricted three-band Gaussian models via AIC and BIC (Fig.~\ref{fig:robustness}e). Along the open-slot vertical stroke $z_d \in [25, 520]\ \mathrm{nm}$ ($27$ positive-force samples $F_z(z_d)>0$ for \texttt{H3-Cantor}), we test for log-periodic modulation in $\ln(F_z z_d^4)$ against a smooth cubic-in-$\ln z_d$ baseline using both fixed-frequency ($\omega_1 = 2\pi/\ln 3 \approx 5.719$) two-harmonic F-tests/AIC/BIC and free-frequency profile Lomb--Scargle\cite{scargle1982} scans over $\omega \in [1,14]$.

\subsection*{3D homothetic \texttt{H3-Cantor}, matched controls, and ablation geometries}
All full-device designs occupy $R \in [3.50, 6.20]\ \mu\mathrm{m}$ across four $C_4$ sectors ($\Delta\phi_{\mathrm{sector}}=90^\circ$, open stator aperture $\Delta\phi_{\mathrm{ap}}=36^\circ$, disengaged open-slot alignment $\theta_{\mathrm{open}}=3^\circ$, engaged landing-pad alignment $\theta_{\mathrm{eng}}=48^\circ$, pad thickness $120\ \mathrm{nm}$). For \texttt{H3-Cantor} (\texttt{H3\_Cantor\_Sym\_2x2x2}, $\Delta\phi_m=0^\circ$) and \texttt{H3-Cantor-Vernier} (\texttt{H3\_Cantor\_Robust}, $\Delta\phi_m \in \{0^\circ, +1.75^\circ, -1.15^\circ\}$), the seven tracks follow $m_k \in (3,2,3,1,3,2,3)$ with $s_m = 3^{3-m} \in \{9,3,1\}$, $W_m = 90\ \mathrm{nm}\cdot s_m$, $w_m = 0.40\,W_m$, $g_{r,m} = 0.30\,W_m \in \{243, 81, 27\}\ \mathrm{nm}$, stator rib widths $(165.0, 97.5, 97.5, 135.0, 135.0, 97.5, 97.5, 165.0)\ \mathrm{nm}$, $z_{\mathrm{stage},m} = -40\ \mathrm{nm}\cdot s_m$, $h_m = 0.70\,|z_{\mathrm{stage},m}|$, $z_{\mathrm{pad,top},m} = -z_{\mathrm{op}} - \frac{1}{2}h_m - 27\ \mathrm{nm}$ ($z_{\mathrm{op}} = 58\ \mathrm{nm}$), $n_{\mathrm{lobe},m} \in \{2,4,8\}$, lobe chords $c_m = 81\ \mathrm{nm}\cdot s_m$, inter-lobe gaps $\{6.0^\circ, 3.6^\circ, 2.1^\circ\}$, and $n_{\mathrm{lobe},m}-1$ suspended micro-bridges of arc widths $\{1.80^\circ, 1.00^\circ, 0.55^\circ\}$ and vertical thicknesses $\{117, 39, 13\}\ \mathrm{nm}$. All five non-fractal controls (\texttt{Ctrl-KnifeEdge-g27}, \texttt{Ctrl-Uni-7Trk}, \texttt{Ctrl-Lin-3Stage}, \texttt{Ctrl-Mono-3Stage}, \texttt{Ctrl-Shuf-OutPhase}), all eight $2^3$ $(I_r,I_\theta,I_z)$ factorial cells, the $N \in \{1,2,3\}$ Cantor recursion-depth ladder, and \texttt{Abl-NoBridges} are analytically area-normalized to $A_{\mathrm{tooth}} = \RobAtoothUmtwoDisp{}\ \mu\mathrm{m}^2$ and discretized with identical $(n_r, n_\phi, n_z, n_{\mathrm{collar}}) = (2,2,2,9)$ sub-panel subdivisions.

\subsection*{Fast $C_4$ Vector-Dyadic BEM, Hermite curved-beam FEM, Laplace $V_{\mathrm{crit}}$, and cleanroom Monte Carlo}
For the $\RobNtotalBemTasks{}$-task robustness and ablation survey, Casimir energies and axial forces at $T=300\ \mathrm{K}$ are evaluated via $C_4$ Bloch-reduced Vector-Dyadic boundary elements ($\ell_{\mathrm{B}} \in \{0,1,2,3\}$) with two tangential surface-current unknowns per panel, Drude--Lorentz gold Fresnel reflection weights ($\omega_p = 9.03\ \mathrm{eV}$, $\gamma = 0.053\ \mathrm{eV}$)\cite{rakic1998}, prime $1/2$ static $n=0$ transverse-magnetic Matsubara weight, and $n_\xi = 20$ Gauss--Legendre tail quadrature. Open-window angular torque ripple is defined over $\theta \in [0^\circ, 6^\circ]$ as $\mathcal{R}_\tau = \min_{\theta \in [0^\circ,6^\circ]} F_z(\theta) / \max_{\theta \in [0^\circ,6^\circ]} F_z(\theta)$ (clipped at $0$ if sign reversal occurs). Stator rib elastostatic stability is solved on every inter-slot rib using a 32-element clamped--clamped curved-beam Euler--Bernoulli finite-element discretization with cubic Hermite shape functions ($E_{\mathrm{Au}} = 79\ \mathrm{GPa}$) loaded by the local radial Maxwell lateral stiffness $\partial F_r/\partial r$ across each unsupported arc span between micro-bridges, yielding the minimum buckling eigenvalue safety factor $\mathrm{SF}_{\mathrm{rib}}$. Mutual rotor--stator electrostatic capacitance $C_{\mathrm{el}}(z)$ and $|\partial C_{\mathrm{el}}/\partial z|$ are computed via 3D Laplace boundary-element collocation on the identical panel mesh to obtain $V_{\mathrm{crit}} = \sqrt{2 F_z(z_{\mathrm{op}}) / |\partial C_{\mathrm{el}}/\partial z|}$. Cleanroom fabrication yield $Y_{\mathrm{lab}}$ is evaluated across $\RobMcChipsPerDesign{}$ independent Monte Carlo chip realizations per design around cleanroom setpoint $z_0 = 75\ \mathrm{nm}$ under joint Gaussian/uniform/Rayleigh priors ($\sigma_{\Delta z}=22\ \mathrm{nm}$, $\sigma_{\Delta w}=8\ \mathrm{nm}$, $\sigma_{\Delta r}=5\ \mathrm{nm}$, $\sigma_{\Delta\theta}=0.45^\circ$, $r_{\mathrm{edge}} \sim \mathcal{U}[2,8]\ \mathrm{nm}$, $\sigma_V = 18\ \mathrm{mV}$), requiring simultaneous non-shorting clearance ($>2\ \mathrm{nm}$), surviving feature width ($>4\ \mathrm{nm}$), absence of rib collapse ($\mathrm{SF}_{\mathrm{rib}}>1$), absence of axial snap-in, and net positive force $F_{z,\mathrm{net}} = F_{z,\mathrm{Cas}} - \frac{1}{2}|\partial C_{\mathrm{el}}/\partial z|V_{\mathrm{patch}}^2 > +0.10\ \mathrm{pN}$.

\subsection*{First-principles 3D Maxwell RWG BEM and four-pass 3D Meep FDTD convergence}
On the shared $20\ \mathrm{nm}$-lattice \texttt{H3-Cantor} unit cell ($4\times$ $C_4$ multiplicity), the rotor lobe spans $[-20,20]\times[-40,40]\times[-80,-40]\ \mathrm{nm}^3$ inside the disjoint closed six-face vacuum Maxwell stress surface $S_{\mathrm{box}} = \partial([-40,40]\times[-80,80]\times[-100,-20]\ \mathrm{nm}^3)$ (maintaining $\ge 20\ \mathrm{nm}$ vacuum standoff from every metal boundary), flanked in the open repulsive state by two stator slot collars $[-80,-60]\times[-120,120]\times[-60,-20]\ \mathrm{nm}^3$ and $[60,80]\times[-120,120]\times[-60,-20]\ \mathrm{nm}^3$ ($40\ \mathrm{nm}$ lateral clearance), or facing the engaged stator landing pad $[-60,60]\times[-100,100]\times[-160,-120]\ \mathrm{nm}^3$ across a $40\ \mathrm{nm}$ vertical gap. In 3D Maxwell RWG BEM\cite{rao1982,reid2013pra}, closed watertight triangulations across Tiers~1--4 ($64 \to 368$ rotor triangles, $124 \to 768$ stator triangles; up to $\ConvOpenPecBemTfourDof{}$ EFIE electric-current unknowns and $\ConvOpenGoldBemTfourDof{}$ coupled electric/magnetic PMCHWT unknowns) are integrated along the Wick-rotated wavenumber axis $\kappa = \xi/c$ ($n_\xi = 16$ baseline, audited up to $n_\xi = 32$) via $F_z = -\frac{\hbar c}{2\pi}\int_0^\infty d\kappa\,\mathrm{Tr}[\mathbf{\Gamma}(\kappa)^{-1}\partial_{z_{\mathrm{rotor}}}\mathbf{\Gamma}(\kappa)]$, with exact analytical $1/R$ singularity extraction on self and edge-sharing triangle pairs and linear $h_{\mathrm{eff}} \to 0$ continuum extrapolation across Tiers~2--4.

In 4-pass 3D Meep FDTD\cite{rodriguez2009pra,oskooi2010}, we disable subpixel permittivity averaging (\texttt{eps\_averaging = False}, Courant factor $0.3$, PML thickness $160\ \mathrm{nm}$, cell buffer $80\ \mathrm{nm}$, $T_{\mathrm{run}} = 4.0\ \mu\mathrm{m}/c$) so both PEC and single-pole Drude gold ($\omega_p = 9.03\ \mathrm{eV}$, $\gamma = 0.053\ \mathrm{eV}$) snap identically to the $20\ \mathrm{nm}$ lattice across $R \in \{100,150,200,250\}\ \mu\mathrm{m}^{-1}$, surrounding the rotor block by a disjoint closed six-face vacuum Maxwell stress surface separated by $20\ \mathrm{nm}$ from both rotor and stator faces. Fluctuating tangential electric and magnetic boundary sources are expanded in DCT basis functions up to multipole cutoff $n_{\max} \in \{1,2,3,4,5\}$ and combined via exact four-pass inclusion--exclusion $F_z^{\mathrm{net}} = (F_z^{\mathrm{both}} - F_z^{\mathrm{rotor}}) - (F_z^{\mathrm{stator}} - F_z^{\mathrm{vac}})$. Joint continuum limits $(\Delta x \to 0, n_{\max}\to\infty)$ combine Richardson spatial extrapolation across $R \in [100,250]\ \mu\mathrm{m}^{-1}$ with the regularized incomplete-gamma 3D Lifshitz multipole completeness fit $F_z(n_{\max}) = F_{z,\infty}\,P(3,\alpha n_{\max}) = F_{z,\infty}[1 - (1 + \alpha n_{\max} + \frac{1}{2}\alpha^2 n_{\max}^2)e^{-\alpha n_{\max}}]$ across $n_{\max}=2,\dots,5$.
