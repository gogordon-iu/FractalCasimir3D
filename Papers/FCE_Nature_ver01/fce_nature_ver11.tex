% Nature Flagship Abstract (strictly <= 200 words; placed before \maketitle in sn-jnl.cls)
\abstract{%
The quantum fluctuation-induced Casimir force between neutral bodies in vacuum is ordinarily attractive, governed by reflection symmetry and parallel-plate overlap. Sidewall fringing between interdigitated ridges and slots can reverse this sign at zero overlap, yet single ridges generate sub-piconewton forces, uniform gratings suffer evanescent screening, and unreinforced nanoscale ribs risk lateral pull-in or snap-in. Here we introduce a three-dimensional homothetic Cantor rotary-axial switch (\texttt{H3-Cantor}) coupling radial middle-third subdivision with generation-scaled azimuthal lobe trains, axial staging, and suspended stator micro-bridges in vacuum. Across matched boundary-element, curved-beam finite-element, six-degree-of-freedom silicon-flexure, and cleanroom Monte Carlo benchmarks against five non-fractal controls, \texttt{H3-Cantor} delivers $+\roundtwo{\HthreeSymFzOpPn}\text{ pN}$ open-slot repulsion over a $\HthreeSymSpanNmDisp{}\text{ nm}$ sign-safe stroke, switches to $\roundtwo{\HthreeSymFzEngagedPn}\text{ pN}$ attraction over stepped landing pads, elevates rib buckling safety above $10^7$ with $\roundone{\HthreeSymYieldPct}\%$ yield, and achieves room-temperature thermomechanical signal-to-noise above $10^4$ in $1\text{ s}$ at Pareto-optimal scale ratio $b=3$. Orthogonal factorial, depth, and bridge ablations isolate radial--azimuthal synergy from stroke stabilization. Finally, five-tier Rao--Wilton--Glisson boundary-element and $\ConvExtTotalFdtdTasks{}$-task four-pass finite-difference time-domain solvers converge quantitatively across single-cell and multiscale Cantor geometries ($\ConvMsGoldGainRatio{}\times$ gold and $\ConvMsPecGainRatio{}\times$ conductor amplification over uniform gratings).}

\maketitle

The Casimir effect---the macroscopic mechanical force generated by boundary-modified quantum electromagnetic fluctuations\cite{casimir1948,casimirpolder1948,bordag2009}---links quantum electrodynamics to the three-dimensional (3D) geometry of matter at sub-micrometre scales\cite{rodriguez2011}. In ordinary vacuum cavities ($\varepsilon_{\mathrm{bg}}=1.0$), both the sign and the spatial range of this interaction are tightly constrained. By the Kenneth--Klich theorem\cite{kennethklich2006}, mirror-symmetric bodies composed of non-magnetic conductors or dielectrics in vacuum attract at all separations, while under the proximity-force approximation (PFA) facing surfaces experience normal attraction proportional to their projected overlap area ($F_{z,\mathrm{face}}^{\mathrm{PFA}} \propto -A_{\mathrm{overlap}}/d^4$). Although repulsive Casimir--Lifshitz forces have been realized through high-index liquid immersion\cite{munday2009}, and vacuum nanomechanics has expanded to Casimir torques\cite{somers2018}, self-assembled polaritonic microcavities\cite{munkhbat2021}, quantum-fluctuation heat transfer\cite{fong2019,Xu2022}, magnetic-field tuning\cite{Zhang2024}, quantum surface softening\cite{zhang2025prl}, and nonlinear superconducting resonators\cite{dejong2026}, amplifying and reversibly switching repulsive Casimir forces in dry vacuum without viscous damping or electrostatic bias has remained an open challenge\cite{rodriguez2011}.

Two distinct geometric principles suggest complementary routes past parallel-plate attraction. First, in electronic and photonic fractals\cite{kempkes2019,xu2021,nuckolls2025} and self-similar plate hierarchies\cite{akkermans2010,shajesh2016,shajesh2017}, discrete scale invariance with ratio $b>1$ replaces continuous scale covariance by a hierarchy of length scales $\ell_m = \ell_1 b^{-(m-1)}$ ($m=1,\dots,N$). Within an effective macroscopic trace framework for self-similar boundaries\cite{milton2004,milton2016,gordon2026prd}, discrete scale symmetry enters through a running Casimir coefficient $C(d_s,\ln(d/\ell_*))$ whose Fourier expansion in $\ln(d/\ell_*)$ carries harmonic frequencies $\omega_k = 2\pi k/\ln b$. Yet in 3D cavities with facing surfaces ($A_{\mathrm{overlap}}>0$), Weyl--Lapidus spectral asymptotics\cite{lapidus1991} dictate that parallel-area terms overwhelm subleading perimeter and multiscale scattering contributions. Second, Levin et al.\cite{levin2010} showed that reflection symmetry in vacuum can be broken geometrically when a narrow metallic ridge sits centred above an open slot aperture ($A_{\mathrm{overlap}}=0$): saddle-point fringing fields pull the ridge toward the slot mid-plane, producing axial repulsion ($+F_z>0$) whose transverse Earnshaw instability can be stabilized by an elastic suspension\cite{levin2010,rodriguez2011}. Scaling this zero-overlap mechanism into a practical 3D actuator, however, confronts four coupled bottlenecks: single ridges generate sub-piconewton forces; dense uniform gratings suffer mutual evanescent screening and lateral rib buckling; single-depth slots exhibit narrow vertical repulsive windows prone to mid-stroke snap-in; and sharp-cornered 3D geometries have lacked quantitative cross-verification between independent time- and frequency-domain Maxwell solvers.

Here we resolve all four bottlenecks simultaneously through a 3D homothetic Cantor rotary-axial Casimir switch (\texttt{H3-Cantor}) in vacuum ($\varepsilon_{\mathrm{bg}}=1.0$; Fig.~\ref{fig:h3_hero} and Extended Data Fig.~\ref{fig:blender_atlas}). Embedding a third-generation ($N=3$, $b=3$) radial Cantor slot-tooth hierarchy into a $C_4$-symmetric annular cavity and scaling azimuthal lobe trains, axial stage elevations, and suspended stator rim micro-bridges homothetically across generations eliminates projected face overlap in the disengaged state ($\theta_{\mathrm{open}}=3^\circ$) to generate multi-piconewton repulsion across a continuous multi-stage vertical stroke, while $45^\circ$ rotation over stepped landing pads ($\theta_{\mathrm{eng}}=48^\circ$) restores parallel-plate overlap and switches the force to multi-piconewton attraction at zero electrical bias. We establish nanomechanical and cleanroom robustness against five area- and gap-matched non-fractal controls (Fig.~\ref{fig:robustness} and Table~\ref{tab:unified_fractal_robustness}), isolate coordinate mechanisms via orthogonal $2^3$ factorial, recursion-depth, and micro-bridge ablations (Fig.~\ref{fig:ablations} and Extended Data Table~\ref{tab:h3_mechanistic_ablations}), verify six-degree-of-freedom (6-DOF) single-crystal silicon suspension stability, rotational trajectories, surface-roughness resilience, and $b=3$ Pareto optimality (Fig.~\ref{fig:stability} and Extended Data Table~\ref{tab:h3_nems_stability}), and demonstrate across single-cell and multiscale benchmarks that five-tier 3D Rao--Wilton--Glisson (RWG) boundary-element\cite{rao1982,reid2013pra} and $\ConvExtTotalFdtdTasks{}$-task four-pass 3D finite-difference time-domain (FDTD)\cite{rodriguez2009pra,oskooi2010} solvers converge quantitatively to the same piconewton Casimir forces (Fig.~\ref{fig:convergence}, Extended Data Fig.~\ref{fig:multiscale_convergence}, and Extended Data Tables~\ref{tab:multiscale-convergence} and~\ref{tab:single-setup-convergence}).

\section*{Homothetic 3D Cantor rotary switch}
\label{sec:architecture}

To motivate coupling radial, azimuthal, and axial scales by a common homothetic ratio $b=3$, consider the effective trace identity for a renormalized self-similar vacuum cavity of characteristic separation $d$ and $N$-th generation cutoff $\ell_* = L b^{-N}$\cite{gordon2026prd}:
\begin{equation}
T^\mu{}_{\mu,\mathrm{vac}} = -\rho_{\mathrm{vac}} + 2P_\parallel + P_\perp = -\frac{\hbar c}{d^4}\,\partial_{\ln d} C\!\left(d_s, \ln\frac{d}{\ell_*}\right),
\label{eq:trace_identity}
\end{equation}
where $\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}$ is the renormalized Casimir energy density and $P_\parallel, P_\perp$ denote transverse and normal pressures. In a 1D transfer-matrix hierarchy $\mathcal{C}_N$ of scale ratio $b$, recursive block partitioning $\Delta e_{\mathcal{C}_N}(d) = 2\Delta e_{\mathcal{C}_{N-1}}(d/b) + \Delta e_{\mathrm{cross}}(d;\ell_*)$ motivates log-spaced characteristic wavenumbers $\kappa_m \propto b^{m-1}$ ($\Delta\ln\kappa = \ln b$). Translating this discrete scale-covering principle into a 3D electromagnetic switch requires extinguishing the leading $A_{\mathrm{overlap}}/d^4$ parallel-plate attraction ($A_{\mathrm{overlap}}=0$) while scaling all three cylindrical coordinates $(r,\theta,z)$ concertedly so coarse, medium, and fine tracks neither screen one another evanescently nor collapse their vertical stroke windows.

Along the azimuthal coordinate ($I_\theta=1$), each track comprises $n_{\mathrm{lobe},m} \in \{2,4,8\}$ rotor lobes ($42$ lobes per sector, $A_{\mathrm{tooth}} = \RobAtoothUmtwoDisp{}\ \mu\mathrm{m}^2$ total) of homothetic chord length $c_m = 81\ \mathrm{nm}\cdot s_m \in \{729, 243, 81\}\ \mathrm{nm}$. Within the inter-lobe angular gaps of every open slot, suspended stator rim micro-bridges (thicknesses $\{117, 39, 13\}\ \mathrm{nm}$, placed at the upper rim for $m=3$ and lower rims for $m=1,2$ so they remain axially disjoint from rotating rotor lobes across all $\theta \in [0^\circ, 90^\circ)$) tie opposing stator ribs together and clamp the maximum unsupported rib arc to $9.10^\circ$. We evaluate both phase-aligned \texttt{H3-Cantor} ($\Delta\phi_m = 0^\circ$) and Vernier phase-staggered \texttt{H3-Cantor-Vernier} ($\Delta\phi_m \in \{0^\circ, +1.75^\circ, -1.15^\circ\}$; Methods).

\section*{Matched controls and lab robustness}
\label{sec:robustness}

To test whether 3D homothetic Cantor organization confers a physical advantage beyond minimum clearance $g_{\min}$ or active footprint $A_{\mathrm{tooth}}$, we benchmark \texttt{H3-Cantor} and \texttt{H3-Cantor-Vernier} against five matched non-fractal controls across the identical annulus $R \in [3.50, 6.20]\ \mu\mathrm{m}$, tooth area $A_{\mathrm{tooth}} = \RobAtoothUmtwoDisp{}\ \mu\mathrm{m}^2$, $300\ \mathrm{K}$ Drude--Lorentz gold response\cite{rakic1998}, and $(2,2,2,9)$ 3D Vector-Dyadic boundary-element mesh ($\HthreeMeshTtwoNr{}$ rotor and $\HthreeMeshTtwoNs{}$ stator panels per sector across $\RobNtotalBemTasks{}$ BEM evaluations; Extended Data Fig.~\ref{fig:blender_atlas} and Methods): \texttt{Ctrl-KnifeEdge-g27} ($19$ coplanar $g_r = 27\ \mathrm{nm}$ tracks with unbridged ribs), \texttt{Ctrl-Uni-7Trk} (uniform 7-track single-stage grating), \texttt{Ctrl-Lin-3Stage} (arithmetic 3-stage spacing), \texttt{Ctrl-Mono-3Stage} (monotonic radial ordering $(3,3,3,3,2,2,1)$ without micro-bridges), and \texttt{Ctrl-Shuf-OutPhase} (radial slot widths misaligned with $(z,\theta)$ stages). Automated Blender mesh audits confirm zero interpenetration, area error $\le \roundthree{\HthreeBlenderMaxAreaErrUmtwo}\ \mu\mathrm{m}^2$, and clearance $\ge \HthreeBlenderMinClearanceNm{}\ \mathrm{nm}$ across all $\HthreeBlenderPassedAudits{}/\HthreeBlenderTotalConfigs{}$ primary designs.

\section*{Factorial and structural ablations}
\label{sec:ablations}

To isolate how each coordinate of \texttt{H3-Cantor} governs force amplification, switching swing, and rib stability, we perform three controlled ablations on the $(2,2,2,9)$ 3D BEM mesh at fixed $A_{\mathrm{tooth}} = \RobAtoothUmtwoDisp{}\ \mu\mathrm{m}^2$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:ablations} and Extended Data Table~\ref{tab:h3_mechanistic_ablations}).

\section*{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Our results demonstrate that 3D homothetic prefractal organization provides an effective design principle for engineering quantum vacuum forces when paired with zero-overlap slot-tooth interdigitation. Eliminating horizontal face overlap ($A_{\mathrm{overlap}}=0$) suppresses the leading attractive Weyl/PFA area term that ordinarily masks boundary hierarchy effects in 3D cavities\cite{kennethklich2006,lapidus1991}, exposing a perimeter- and sidewall-dominated scattering regime\cite{levin2010}. Within that window, radial Cantor scaling and generation-scaled azimuthal lobe segmentation act synergistically ($\mathcal{I}_{r\theta} = \AblYatesOpenIrth{}\ \mathrm{pN}$) to pack high sidewall-proximity density without the evanescent self-screening or rib-buckling fragility of dense uniform gratings, while homothetic axial staging at the Pareto-optimal ratio $b=3$ links three generation-resolved repulsive basins into a $\HthreeSymSpanNmDisp{}\ \mathrm{nm}$ sign-safe stroke and suspended rim micro-bridges stiffen stator ribs $\AblBridgeSfGainRatio{}$-fold. Coupling \texttt{H3-Cantor} to monolithic $\mathrm{Si}\langle 110\rangle$ flexures ensures global 6-DOF Earnshaw stability ($\mathrm{SF}_r, \mathrm{SF}_{\mathrm{tilt}} \gg 1$), $\StabTiltTwomradRetPctDisp{}\%$ tilt retention at $2\ \mathrm{mrad}$, and $>10^4$ room-temperature lock-in SNR within $1\ \mathrm{s}$, while quantitative convergence between five-tier RWG BEM and $\ConvExtTotalFdtdTasks{}$-task four-pass 3D FDTD across single-cell and multiscale geometries ($\ConvMsGoldGainRatio{}\times$ gold and $\ConvMsPecGainRatio{}\times$ PEC amplification) establishes a first-principles blueprint for cleanroom-fabricable vacuum Casimir switches, stiction-free nanoelectromechanical clutches, and zero-bias quantum-fluctuation sensors\cite{safronova2018,lodahl2015}.

\section*{Methods}

\subsection*{1D self-similar transfer-matrix heuristic and spectral/stroke analysis}
In the motivating 1D scalar hierarchy $\mathcal{C}_N$ of scale factor $b=3$\cite{milton2004,gordon2026prd}, each prefractal generation subdivides intervals by $1/b$, motivating generation-indexed characteristic imaginary wavenumbers $\kappa_m \sim g_{r,m}^{-1} \propto b^{m-1}$. In 3D BEM, we extract the generation-resolved coupling spectrum $w_m(\kappa)$ ($m \in \{1,2,3\}$) across a hybrid logarithmic grid of $273$ total $(m,\kappa)$ samples by isolating radial-sidewall panels belonging to generation $m$, fitting local quadratic peaks $\ln\hat{\kappa}_m$ around each sub-band maximum, and comparing exact $\ln 3$-spaced, compressed-periodic, and unrestricted three-band Gaussian models via AIC and BIC (Fig.~\ref{fig:robustness}e). Along the open-slot vertical stroke $z_d \in [25, 520]\ \mathrm{nm}$ ($27$ positive-force samples $F_z(z_d)>0$ for \texttt{H3-Cantor}), we test for log-periodic modulation in $\ln(F_z z_d^4)$ against a smooth cubic-in-$\ln z_d$ baseline using both fixed-frequency ($\omega_1 = 2\pi/\ln 3 \approx 5.719$) two-harmonic F-tests/AIC/BIC and free-frequency profile Lomb--Scargle\cite{scargle1982} scans over $\omega \in [1,14]$.

\subsection*{3D homothetic \texttt{H3-Cantor}, matched controls, and ablation geometries}
All full-device designs occupy $R \in [3.50, 6.20]\ \mu\mathrm{m}$ across four $C_4$ sectors ($\Delta\phi_{\mathrm{sector}}=90^\circ$, open stator aperture $\Delta\phi_{\mathrm{ap}}=36^\circ$, disengaged open-slot alignment $\theta_{\mathrm{open}}=3^\circ$, engaged landing-pad alignment $\theta_{\mathrm{eng}}=48^\circ$, pad thickness $120\ \mathrm{nm}$). For \texttt{H3-Cantor} (\texttt{H3\_Cantor\_Sym\_2x2x2}, $\Delta\phi_m=0^\circ$) and \texttt{H3-Cantor-Vernier} (\texttt{H3\_Cantor\_Robust}, $\Delta\phi_m \in \{0^\circ, +1.75^\circ, -1.15^\circ\}$), the seven tracks follow $m_k \in (3,2,3,1,3,2,3)$ with $s_m = 3^{3-m} \in \{9,3,1\}$, $W_m = 90\ \mathrm{nm}\cdot s_m$, $w_m = 0.40\,W_m$, $g_{r,m} = 0.30\,W_m \in \{243, 81, 27\}\ \mathrm{nm}$, stator rib widths $(165.0, 97.5, 97.5, 135.0, 135.0, 97.5, 97.5, 165.0)\ \mathrm{nm}$, $z_{\mathrm{stage},m} = -40\ \mathrm{nm}\cdot s_m$, $h_m = 0.70\,|z_{\mathrm{stage},m}|$, $z_{\mathrm{pad,top},m} = -z_{\mathrm{op}} - \frac{1}{2}h_m - 27\ \mathrm{nm}$ ($z_{\mathrm{op}} = 58\ \mathrm{nm}$), $n_{\mathrm{lobe},m} \in \{2,4,8\}$, lobe chords $c_m = 81\ \mathrm{nm}\cdot s_m$, inter-lobe gaps $\{6.0^\circ, 3.6^\circ, 2.1^\circ\}$, and $n_{\mathrm{lobe},m}-1$ suspended micro-bridges of arc widths $\{1.80^\circ, 1.00^\circ, 0.55^\circ\}$ and vertical thicknesses $\{117, 39, 13\}\ \mathrm{nm}$. All five non-fractal controls (\texttt{Ctrl-KnifeEdge-g27}, \texttt{Ctrl-Uni-7Trk}, \texttt{Ctrl-Lin-3Stage}, \texttt{Ctrl-Mono-3Stage}, \texttt{Ctrl-Shuf-OutPhase}), all eight $2^3$ $(I_r,I_\theta,I_z)$ factorial cells, the $N \in \{1,2,3\}$ Cantor recursion-depth ladder, and \texttt{Abl-NoBridges} are analytically area-normalized to $A_{\mathrm{tooth}} = \RobAtoothUmtwoDisp{}\ \mu\mathrm{m}^2$ and discretized with identical $(n_r, n_\phi, n_z, n_{\mathrm{collar}}) = (2,2,2,9)$ sub-panel subdivisions.

\subsection*{Fast $C_4$ Vector-Dyadic BEM, Hermite curved-beam FEM, Laplace $V_{\mathrm{crit}}$, and cleanroom Monte Carlo}
For the $\RobNtotalBemTasks{}$-task robustness and ablation survey, Casimir energies and axial forces at $T=300\ \mathrm{K}$ are evaluated via $C_4$ Bloch-reduced Vector-Dyadic boundary elements ($\ell_{\mathrm{B}} \in \{0,1,2,3\}$) with two tangential surface-current unknowns per panel, Drude--Lorentz gold Fresnel reflection weights ($\omega_p = 9.03\ \mathrm{eV}$, $\gamma = 0.053\ \mathrm{eV}$)\cite{rakic1998}, prime $1/2$ static $n=0$ transverse-magnetic Matsubara weight, and $n_\xi = 20$ Gauss--Legendre tail quadrature. Dimensionless open-window parasitic torque-to-lift ratio is evaluated across the interior alignment stencil $\theta \in [1.5^\circ, 4.5^\circ]$ around $\theta_{\mathrm{open}} = 3^\circ$ as $\mathcal{R}_\tau = \max_{\theta \in [1.5^\circ, 4.5^\circ]} |\tau_\theta(\theta)| / [R_{\mathrm{mean}}\,\max(|F_z(\theta_{\mathrm{open}})|, 0.10\ \mathrm{pN})]$ with $R_{\mathrm{mean}} = \frac{1}{2}(R_{\min} + R_{\max}) = 4.85\ \mu\mathrm{m}$. Stator rib elastostatic stability is solved on every inter-slot rib using a 32-element clamped--clamped curved-beam Euler--Bernoulli finite-element discretization with cubic Hermite shape functions ($E_{\mathrm{Au}} = 79\ \mathrm{GPa}$) loaded by the local radial Maxwell lateral stiffness $\partial F_r/\partial r$ across each unsupported arc span between micro-bridges, yielding the minimum buckling eigenvalue safety factor $\mathrm{SF}_{\mathrm{rib}}$. Mutual rotor--stator electrostatic capacitance $C_{\mathrm{el}}(z)$ and $|\partial C_{\mathrm{el}}/\partial z|$ are computed via 3D Laplace boundary-element collocation on the identical panel mesh to obtain $V_{\mathrm{crit}} = \sqrt{2 F_z(z_{\mathrm{op}}) / |\partial C_{\mathrm{el}}/\partial z|}$. Cleanroom fabrication yield $Y_{\mathrm{lab}}$ is evaluated across $\RobMcChipsPerDesign{}$ independent Monte Carlo chip realizations per design around cleanroom setpoint $z_0 = 75\ \mathrm{nm}$ under joint Gaussian/uniform/Rayleigh priors ($\sigma_{\Delta z}=22\ \mathrm{nm}$, $\sigma_{\Delta w}=8\ \mathrm{nm}$, $\sigma_{\Delta r}=5\ \mathrm{nm}$, $\sigma_{\Delta\theta}=0.45^\circ$, $r_{\mathrm{edge}} \sim \mathcal{U}[2,8]\ \mathrm{nm}$, $\sigma_V = 18\ \mathrm{mV}$), requiring simultaneous non-shorting clearance ($>2\ \mathrm{nm}$), surviving feature width ($>4\ \mathrm{nm}$), absence of rib collapse ($\mathrm{SF}_{\mathrm{rib}}>1$), absence of axial snap-in, and net positive force $F_{z,\mathrm{net}} = F_{z,\mathrm{Cas}} - \frac{1}{2}|\partial C_{\mathrm{el}}/\partial z|V_{\mathrm{patch}}^2 > +0.10\ \mathrm{pN}$.

\subsection*{6-DOF $\mathrm{Si}\langle 110\rangle$ suspension, non-parallel four-sector tilt, dispersion/roughness, and $b$-scan suite}
Across the $\StabNtotalBemTasks{}$-task stability suite, four folded single-crystal $\mathrm{Si}\langle 110\rangle$ flexure tethers ($E_{\mathrm{Si}\langle 110\rangle} = 169\ \mathrm{GPa}$, $\rho_{\mathrm{Si}} = 2329\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$, rotor effective mass $m_{\mathrm{eff}} = \StabMeffPgDisp{}\ \mathrm{pg}$) are synthesized across Isometric (Regime~I), nm-Stroke (Regime~II), and Long-Stroke (Regime~III) beam aspect ratios, verifying radial Earnshaw safety $\mathrm{SF}_r = k_{r,\mathrm{mech}} / |\partial F_r/\partial r|_{\mathrm{Cas}} > 1$ and tilt-rocking safety $\mathrm{SF}_{\mathrm{tilt}} = \kappa_{\mathrm{tilt,mech}} / |\kappa_{\mathrm{tilt,Cas}}| > 1$. Out-of-plane rotor tilt $\alpha_{\mathrm{tilt}} \in [0, 2.5]\ \mathrm{mrad}$ breaks $C_4$ cyclic symmetry and is cross-verified at $\alpha_{\mathrm{tilt}} \in \{0, 1.0, 2.0\}\ \mathrm{mrad}$ via coupled four-sector 3D BEM without Bloch reduction. Room-temperature ($T = 300\ \mathrm{K}$) Brownian force noise spectral density $S_F^{1/2} = \sqrt{4 k_{\mathrm{B}} T m_{\mathrm{eff}} \omega_z / Q}$ determines lock-in signal-to-noise $\mathrm{SNR}(\tau) = F_z(\theta_{\mathrm{open}})\sqrt{2\tau} / S_F^{1/2}$ for $Q \in \{10^4, 10^5\}$. Dielectric sensitivity compares ideal $0\ \mathrm{K}$ PEC, $0\ \mathrm{K}$ and $300\ \mathrm{K}$ 1-pole Drude gold, and full 6-pole Raki\'c Lorentz--Drude gold\cite{rakic1998} (Matsubara pole truncation audited between $N_M = 80$ and $160$), while uncorrelated rotor and stator RMS film roughness $\sigma_{\mathrm{rough}} \in [0, 3.0]\ \mathrm{nm}$ ($\sigma_{\mathrm{gap}}^2 = 2\sigma_{\mathrm{rough}}^2$) is propagated via second-order axial and radial gap-curvature averaging $\delta F_z = \frac{1}{2}(\partial_{z_d}^2 F_z + \partial_{g_r}^2 F_z)\sigma_{\mathrm{gap}}^2$ extracted from the solved 3D BEM stroke and etch-bias curves (and dispersive $d^{-3.6}$ parallel-face curvature across the $27\ \mathrm{nm}$ engaged landing pad). Homothetic scale-ratio scans $b \in \{2.50, 2.75, 3.00, 3.25, 3.50, 3.75\}$ hold annular bounds $R \in [3.50, 6.20]\ \mu\mathrm{m}$, minimum clearance $g_{\min} = 27\ \mathrm{nm}$, and $A_{\mathrm{tooth}} = \RobAtoothUmtwoDisp{}\ \mu\mathrm{m}^2$ fixed.

\subsection*{Five-tier 3D Maxwell RWG BEM, $\ConvExtTotalFdtdTasks{}$-task four-pass 3D Meep FDTD, and multiscale benchmark}
On the shared $20\ \mathrm{nm}$-lattice \texttt{H3-Cantor} single-setup unit cell ($4\times$ $C_4$ multiplicity), the rotor lobe spans $[-20,20]\times[-40,40]\times[-80,-40]\ \mathrm{nm}^3$ inside the disjoint closed six-face vacuum Maxwell stress surface $S_{\mathrm{box}} = \partial([-40,40]\times[-80,80]\times[-100,-20]\ \mathrm{nm}^3)$ ($\ge 20\ \mathrm{nm}$ vacuum standoff from every metal boundary), flanked in the open repulsive state by stator slot collars $[-80,-60]\times[-120,120]\times[-60,-20]\ \mathrm{nm}^3$ and $[60,80]\times[-120,120]\times[-60,-20]\ \mathrm{nm}^3$ ($40\ \mathrm{nm}$ lateral clearance), or facing the engaged stator landing pad $[-60,60]\times[-100,100]\times[-160,-120]\ \mathrm{nm}^3$ across a $40\ \mathrm{nm}$ vertical gap. On the $20\ \mathrm{nm}$-lattice three-track multiscale benchmark, \texttt{Multiscale-Cantor-3Trk} places staged $40/80/40\ \mathrm{nm}$-wide rotor teeth inside $120/240/120\ \mathrm{nm}$ open stator slots ($A_{\mathrm{tooth,cell}} = 0.0128\ \mu\mathrm{m}^2$ and total open slot width $480\ \mathrm{nm}$ per sector), matched identically in active tooth area and total open aperture against \texttt{Ctrl-Uniform-Coarse2} (two $80\ \mathrm{nm}$ teeth in $240\ \mathrm{nm}$ slots). In 3D Maxwell RWG BEM\cite{rao1982,reid2013pra}, closed watertight triangulations across Tiers~1--5 on the single-setup cell (up to $\ConvExtOpenPecBemTfiveDof{}$ EFIE and $\ConvExtOpenGoldBemTfiveDof{}$ PMCHWT unknowns) and Tiers~1--3 on the multiscale pair are integrated along $\kappa = \xi/c$ ($n_\xi = 16$ baseline, audited up to $n_\xi = 32$) via $F_z = -\frac{\hbar c}{2\pi}\int_0^\infty d\kappa\,\mathrm{Tr}[\mathbf{\Gamma}(\kappa)^{-1}\partial_{z_{\mathrm{rotor}}}\mathbf{\Gamma}(\kappa)]$ with exact Wilton $1/R$ singularity extraction and linear $h_{\mathrm{eff}} \to 0$ continuum extrapolation across the finest three tiers.

In 4-pass 3D Meep FDTD\cite{rodriguez2009pra,oskooi2010}, subpixel permittivity averaging is disabled (\texttt{eps\_averaging = False}, Courant factor $0.5$, baseline PML thickness $160\ \mathrm{nm}$, cell buffer $80\ \mathrm{nm}$, $T_{\mathrm{run}} = 4.0\ \mu\mathrm{m}/c$; audited at $T_{\mathrm{run}} = 8.0\ \mu\mathrm{m}/c$, $140\ \mathrm{nm}$ buffer, and $240\ \mathrm{nm}$ PML) so both PEC and single-pole Drude gold ($\omega_p = 9.03\ \mathrm{eV}$, $\gamma = 0.053\ \mathrm{eV}$) snap identically to the $20\ \mathrm{nm}$ lattice across $R \in \{100,150,200,250\}\ \mu\mathrm{m}^{-1}$. Fluctuating tangential boundary sources expanded up to DCT order $n_{\max} \in \{1,2,3,4,5\}$ are combined via $F_z^{\mathrm{net}} = (F_z^{\mathrm{both}} - F_z^{\mathrm{rotor}}) - (F_z^{\mathrm{stator}} - F_z^{\mathrm{vac}})$, with continuum limits combining matched-$n_{\max}=4$ spatial Richardson $\Delta x \to 0$ extrapolation across $R \in \{150,200,250\}\ \mu\mathrm{m}^{-1}$ and $R=200\ \mu\mathrm{m}^{-1}$ incomplete-gamma 3D Lifshitz multipole completion $F_z(n_{\max}) = F_{z,\infty}\,P(3,\alpha n_{\max})$. All $\ConvExtTotalFdtdTasks{}$ post-bugfix four-pass 3D FDTD tasks ($31$ baseline single-setup tasks plus all $20$ extended coupled high-$(R,n_{\max})$, domain-invariance, and $R \in \{100,150\}$ multiscale PEC and Drude gold tasks) completed to full multipole moment convergence.
